## Reading notes (one per claim)

- **(a) Flat Earth — refuted, >99.99 %.** Five *methodologically independent* determinations of Earth's radius (lunar horizontal parallax; geodetic spherical excess; horizon dip; the WGS 84 meridional quadrant; the lunar-eclipse umbra) agree to within 7.137 km (0.112 %) using **one** free parameter. A planar model has no such parameter and must add at least one ad-hoc free parameter per observation — the signature of an unfalsifiable hypothesis. **Turn 7 adds an eleventh test:** the four southern non-stop routes imply ground speeds of 745–886 km/h in a single band, while the standard north-centred azimuthal-equidistant flat map over-predicts the Sydney–Santiago distance by 2.26–3.00× and would need >1,600 km/h of cruise speed where ~900 km/h is flown.
- **(b) Faked Moon landing — refuted, >99.9 %.** The decisive evidence is positive and repeatable *today*: five retroreflector arrays (Apollo 11/14/15 plus the French-built arrays on the Soviet Lunokhod 1/2 rovers) return pulses at 2.378–2.713 s; 382 kg of samples in laboratories worldwide are radiometrically dated 4.4–4.5 Ga and matched to the Soviet Luna 16/20/24 returns; the crew doses were *measured in flight* (Apollo 11 = 0.18 rad skin, against a 400-rad operational limit); later missions (Chang'e-5) extend rather than contradict the geochemical framework. **Turn 8 adds the external dimension: the third-party evidence is no longer a paragraph of assertions but a 13-nation list with instruments and dates, capped by the Soviet RATAN-600 radio telescope measuring all five ALSEP transmitters in Oct–Nov 1977 at their published selenographic coordinates and 20 W power (Naugolnaia et al., *Soviet Astronomy Letters* 4:302, 1978) — and by ESRO's TD-1A satellite matching the star field in the Apollo 16 far-UV frames, which also kills the strong "no stars anywhere" version of the anomaly.**
- **(c) Roswell / Area 51 — documented part true, extraordinary part unestablished, 85–95 % mundane (raised turn 9 from 71–99.5 %).** Area 51, Project Mogul and the 1994/1997 USAF reports are real and sourced. The alien-craft/bodies claim has no specimen, no chain of custody, no peer-reviewed isotope-geochemistry publication and no independent reference-lab confirmation. **Turn 9 changed the *grade* of the evidence, not the direction of the verdict:** reading the primary USAF reports in full showed that the 1947 *debris* is not merely unexplained-by-alternative, it is *positively measured* as a shredded Mogul train — 2.268 kg of rubber, paper, tape and sticks over 182.88 m, an areal density of 0.086 g/m², with "no sign of any metal … for an engine and no sign of any propellers," and a material inventory that matches the documented target construction item for item. **The absence-of-evidence flag narrows but does not vanish:** it now applies only to "no alien craft has ever been demonstrated to exist anywhere," not to the 1947 debris, which is the claim the brief asks about. A true skeptic could still hold 60–80 %, but only by assigning the measured debris inventory and the aircraft-crash control essentially zero weight.
- **(d) Alien abduction — experiences real (~99 %), extraterrestrial cause unsupported (~95 % reported; arithmetic grade >99.99 %).** Isolated sleep paralysis (lifetime prevalence ~6–8 % general, 28 % students, 32 % psychiatric inpatients) produces the same phenomenology, abduction reporters show elevated false recall, and the 'grey' template tracks contemporaneous media. The evidence is **predominantly absent rather than disconfirming**: no measurement can positively exclude an advanced non-terrestrial actor, so this verdict is a weaker evidentiary grade than (a) and (b). **Turn 8 adds the first *positive* naturalistic result: a 2021 cohort instructed to generate alien encounters in REM sleep succeeded 114/~152 = 75 % of the time, with sleep paralysis and fear appearing in the ~23 'close to reality' cases; and the prevalence arithmetic now shows the sleep-paralysis pool (6.2–8 %) fully contains the 2–6 % claimant population (ratio 0.25–0.97), so no unmeasured population is needed. The paper itself remains second-hand here (journal site behind an anti-bot challenge, ResearchGate 403).**
