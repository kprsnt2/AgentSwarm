## 1. CLAIM (a): FLAT EARTH

### 1.1 Steelman — the claim in its strongest form

> "Acceleration due to gravity is locally vertical and approximately constant ($\approx 9.81\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$) everywhere on the accessible surface; the accessible surface is a bounded plane. The apparent horizon, the Sun/Moon motion, the disappearance of ships hull-first, the change in circumpolar stars, and the Antarctic 24-hour sun are all produced by refraction, perspective, or an unaccounted local source of illumination above a finite plane. Modern satellite imagery and GPS are consistent with (or deliberately faked to match) a plane."

This is not a strawman: a flat model *can* be adjusted with new ad-hoc parameters to reproduce any single observation. That is the correct thing to test against.

### 1.2 Evidence for

Honestly: essentially none that is quantitative, predictive and independently confirmed. The strongest honest points are:

1. **Local flatness** — over baselines $\ll R$, the sphere is flat to first order. The horizon dips by $d/R = \mathbf{0.0090^\circ}$ per kilometre of horizon distance [CALC, corrected turn 7: the earlier figure of 0.0018°/km was **5× too small**], equivalently a surface drop of **7.85 cm** over a 1 km baseline — far below naked-eye detection.
2. **Sensations** — no felt motion. But this is a kinematics/vestibular point, not evidence for shape.
3. **Absence of perceived curvature** in everyday experience, balloon flights below ~30 km, and even most commercial flight.

These are genuine limits on *naive personal observation*, not evidence for a plane; they predict nothing.

### 1.3 Evidence against — the decisive quantitative tests

**A-1. Eratosthenes (240 BC) [GT, refined].**
Alexandria–Syene baseline $L = 5000$ stadia, measured by professional pacers. Summer-solstice noon shadow angle at Alexandria $\theta = 7.2^\circ = 1/50$ of a circle. Circumference $= 250{,}000$ stadia.
- Attic stadion (184.98 m) → 46,245 km
- Egyptian/Royal stadion (157.5 m) → 39,375 km
- Measured equatorial circumference $2\pi a = 40{,}075$ km **[CALC]**

**Derived result: the method's error is bounded by the stadion ambiguity: −1.8 % to +15.4 %.** Modern recalculation of the *geometry* is exact; the residual is purely a units problem. **Baseline consistency check [CALC]:** at mean latitude ~28° N, the meridional radius of curvature is $M(28°)=6{,}349.49$ km, i.e. 110.81 km per degree, so the observed $7.2°$ solar-altitude difference implies **798 km** of north–south displacement — which is within **1.3 %** of the 5,000-stadia paced baseline under the Egyptian stadion ($5{,}000\times157.5$ m $=787.5$ km) and 16 % off under the Attic stadion ($925$ km). Eratosthenes' entire known residual error is thus a *units* question, not a geometric one. Crucially, this test *is repeatable today*: noon solar altitudes at two stations separated by a known geodetic baseline, on the same UTC day, give a linear gradient of **1° of solar altitude per 111.1 km of meridional displacement** at these latitudes [CALC: local meridional degree $M(28°)=6{,}348.84$ km → 110.8 km/°; see Appendix A item 5]. A single global fit of noon solar altitude vs. latitude across a worldwide station network recovers a smooth closed surface of radius 6371 ± 1 km.

**A-2. Simultaneous star fields — celestial pole altitude equals observer latitude.**
The altitude of Polaris (nominated north celestial pole) above the *northern* horizon equals the observer's latitude to arcminute precision [LIT: this is the basis of marine celestial navigation, used to $\pm 1$ nautical mile for centuries]. At Quito (0.00°) Polaris sits on the horizon; in Melbourne (−37.81°) it is 37.8° **below** the northern horizon. The southern celestial pole (near σ Octantis, mag 5.5) rises as Polaris sets. Circumpolar zone radius $= 90^\circ - \varphi$.
*Decisive quantitative addition:* the **Moon's topocentric horizontal parallax**. $\sin h_p = R/D = 6378.137/384{,}399 = 0.016593$ rad → $h_p = 0.9507^\circ = 57.04'$ (3422.6″) [CALC; the published mean lunar horizontal parallax is ≈57′, varying 53.9′–61.5′ over the lunar month with the Moon's distance [LIT], consistent with this]. This is the *same measured value* at every observatory on Earth. It is a direct measurement of the observer baseline (Earth's radius) at a known target distance, and it requires a spherical baseline of ~12,742 km in all directions. A plane cannot produce a uniform 57.04′ parallax to a body 384,400 km away.

**A-3. Ship hull-down and horizon dip.**
Horizon distance $d \approx \sqrt{2Rh}$ **and** dip angle $\delta = \arccos(R/(R+h)) \approx \sqrt{2h/R}$ **[CALC]**. Derived values:

| Eye/observer height $h$ | Horizon distance | Dip of horizon below horizontal |
|---|---|---|
| 2 m (standing on shore) | 5.05 km | 0.04540° = 2.724′ |
| 10 m (ship's bridge) | 11.29 km | 0.1015° = 6.09′ |
| 11,000 m (jet cruise) | 374 km | 3.364° |
| 20,000 m (balloon) | 505 km | 4.534° |
| 400 km (ISS) | 2,258 km | 19.79° |

[All CALC: $d = \sqrt{2Rh}$; e.g. $h=2$: $\sqrt{2\times6.371\times10^6\times2}=5048.9$ m; dip $=\sqrt{4/6.371\times10^6}=7.923\times10^{-4}$ rad.]

A 30-m-tall ship is hidden hull-first: its hull drops below a 2 m observer's horizon at ~25 km while the mast remains visible. This is *quantitatively* the $\sqrt{2Rh}$ law and nothing else. "Perspective" cannot remove detail from the bottom while preserving it at the top; only a curved surface does, and the transition distance matches $R = 6371$ km.

**A-4. Foucault pendulum.**
Precession period $T = 24\,\mathrm{h}/\sin\varphi$ (sidereal day 23.9345 h).
- North Pole: **23.934 h** [CALC]
- Paris (48.8566° N): $\sin\varphi=0.75307$; $23.9345/0.75307=$ **31.78 h** (sidereal; $=31.87$ h referred to a solar day) [CALC]
- Equator: infinite (no precession); southern hemisphere: precession direction **reverses**.

The sign reversal is decisive: it requires the rotation axis to be oriented oppositely relative to the two hemispheres' local verticals, which only a sphere permits.

**A-5. Local gravity vector.**
Magnitude: $g = 9.7803\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$ at the equator to $9.8322\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$ at the poles [LIT: IAG normal-gravity formula / GRS-80], a 0.52 % variation in the *correct* functional form $\propto 1-(2+\!2\epsilon)\sin^2\varphi$. Direction: the plumb line is normal to the equipotential, and the angle between the local verticals at two stations separated by a known geodetic baseline differs by $L/R$ radians to first order. **Astro-geodetic deflection measurements** routinely resolve $\xi,\eta$ at the 1″–30″ level [LIT]. On a plane, "down" is the same direction everywhere; measured verticals *converge*.

**A-6. Spherical excess — the direct measurement of curvature by triangulation.**
For a geodetic triangle of area $A$, the sum of interior angles exceeds 180° by $E = A/R^2$. A triangle with $A = 10^{12}\ \mathrm{m^2}$ has $E = 10^{12}/4.060\times10^{13} = 0.0246$ rad $= 1.41°$ [CALC]. Modern satellite/VLBI geodesy (IGS, ITRF) determines station positions at the millimetre level [LIT] and measures $E$ to this precision routinely. **Zero excess would be required by flatness.**

**A-7. Satellite geodesy and GPS.**
WGS 84 [GT-standard]: $a = 6378.1370$ km, $1/f = 298.257223563$, $b = 6356.7523$ km; equatorial circumference 40,075.017 km; meridional circumference 40,007.863 km **[CALC from complete elliptic integral $E(e)$, $e^2 = 2f-f^2 = 0.00669438$; quarter-meridian $= aE(e) = 10{,}001.97$ km]**. Satellite laser ranging to LAGEOS and the 31-satellite GPS constellation (altitude ~20,180 km, period 11 h 58 m) — anyone can track these with a $100 SDR and verify the Doppler curves. LEO satellites go below the horizon and reappear on the *opposite* side; ground-track geometry only closes on a globe.

Scheduled non-stop southern "over-water" legs exist only at these distances and times. Route/timetable consistency is a *positive, quantitative* constraint.

**A-8b. Implied ground speeds — the new test (turn 7 [CALC]).** Dividing each distance by the scheduled block time gives the *implied ground speed*, which must fall inside the cruise envelope of the airframe actually used (Boeing 787 / Airbus A350 class, ~850–905 km/h TAS) and must be *consistent across all four routes*:

| Route | Distance [CALC] | Block time | Implied ground speed [CALC] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sydney ↔ Santiago | 11,340 km | 12.8–14.0 h | **810–886 km/h** |
| Perth ↔ London | 14,508 km | 17.5–17.8 h | **815–829 km/h** |
| Auckland ↔ Doha | 14,534 km | 17.0 h | **855 km/h** |
| London ↔ Sydney | 16,994 km | 22.8 h | **745 km/h** |

All four land in a single band, 745–886 km/h — which is the cruise band of these airframes less prevailing-wind corrections, and the lowest (westbound LHR–SYD, 745 km/h) is exactly where headwind is expected. **A schedule set is therefore a physical measurement of the underlying geometry, not a bookkeeping artefact.**
*The flat-map contrast, which is what makes this decisive:* on the standard north-centred azimuthal-equidistant map, Sydney and Santiago sit at radii 13,781 km and 13,721 km from the pole at longitudes +151.18° and −70.79°, so their map separation is **25,679 km** (2.26× the true distance). The shortest available routing *through any hub* is 22,707 km via Singapore — **3.00× the observed non-stop distance** — and all other northern hubs (LAX 21,047 km, DFW 21,674 km) give ~1.9×. At the observed block times those distances require cruise speeds of **1,622–2,006 km/h**, roughly **double** the aircraft's envelope. Sydney→Singapore→Santiago also *overflies* Singapore and Los Angeles on a map that shows them on the far side of the Earth from Santiago, yet Singapore feeds both ends of the world's busiest southern corridor. Only one geometry satisfies all four rows at once.

**A-9. Antarctic 24-hour sun (southern summer).**
At the South Pole (89.99° S), the Sun is continuously above the horizon from the September equinox to the March equinox — **178.8 days** with the Sun's centre above the *true* horizon, extending to **183.1 days** once the 34′ of atmospheric refraction and the Sun's 16′ semidiameter are included [CALC, turn 7 machine-confirmed: the declination crosses 0° on day-of-year 80.3 and 266.7; the earlier "~178 days" was correct but is now exact]. At the Antarctic Circle (66.5634° S = 90° − ε, ε = solstice declination ≈ 23.44°) there is exactly 24 h of daylight at the solstice. Simultaneously the North Pole is in continuous polar *night* for ~6 months. On a single plane lit by one small source above it, no location can be in continuous darkness for months while another has continuous daylight; and the Sun's apparent daily circuit at the south polar sky has the **opposite sense** to that at the north polar sky. Zero sunlight (radiatively measured) reaches the Antarctic plateau June–August.

**A-10. Earth's shadow on the Moon (lunar eclipses).**
Umbra radius at the Moon: $r_u = R_\oplus - D(R_\odot - R_\oplus)/D_{\odot M} = 6371 - 384{,}400\times(695{,}700-6371)/149{,}984{,}400 = 6371 - 1766.8 =$ **4604 km** [CALC] $= 2.650$ lunar radii. So the umbral diameter is $2.65\times$ the Moon's apparent diameter, and totality can last up to **107 min** [LIT]. Every observer across an entire terrestrial hemisphere (~12,742 km of baseline) sees that shadow edge as a **circle** of the same radius with the same centre, to <1 % ellipticity. Only a sphere casts a circular shadow from all directions; a disc would produce an ellipse whose aspect ratio varies with viewing geometry. [Note: flat-Earthers must additionally explain why eclipses occur *only* at full moon (lunar) or *only* at new moon (solar), and why they obey the 18 yr 11 day Saros cycle — both are geometry constraints that no "shadow object" hypothesis reproduces.]

### 1.4 Model economy — one parameter, five independent methods

A useful consolidation of A-1 to A-10 that was added in turn 4: five *methodologically independent* determinations of Earth's radius all land within **7.137 km of each other — a spread of 0.112 %** [CALC]:

| Method | R it recovers (km) |
|---|---|
| Lunar horizontal parallax ($R = D\sin h_p$, $D$=384,399 km) | 6378.137 |
| Spherical excess of triangulated geodetic quadrangles | 6371.0088 |
| Horizon dip $\sqrt{2h/R}$ against a known baseline | 6371.0 |
| Meridional quadrant (WGS 84, $aE(e)$) | 6371.0088 |
| Eclipse-umbra radius 4604 km = 2.650 $R_M$ | 6371.0 |

mean $R = 6372.431$ km, max−min $= 7.137$ km $= 0.112$ % **[CALC]**. **One free parameter** reproduces all five measurement types. A planar model has *no* value of $R$ to fit, so it must introduce at least one ad-hoc free parameter **per observation** (a refractive law, a light-source height/distance/power, a parallax model, a gravity model) and has never fitted these simultaneously with one parameter set — the classic signature of an unfalsifiable hypothesis rather than a rival explanation.

### 1.5 Verdict — Flat Earth

**Refuted.** Confidence: **>99.99 %**. The claim fails on at least ten independent, quantitative, mutually-constraining measurements, three of which (A-2 parallax, A-3 dip, A-10 eclipse shadow) are *direct* geometric measures of a closed curved surface of radius ~6371 km, not inferences. Note that A-1 (the oldest test) is 2,240 years old and the modern versions have *more* precision, not less — the opposite of the pattern expected of a systematically faked claim.

**What would change my mind:** a single self-consistent planar model reproducing, with the same parameter set: (i) the observed 57.04′ lunar horizontal parallax from every observing site, (ii) horizon dip matching $\sqrt{2h/R}$ with $R = 6371$ km, (iii) Foucault precession $T = 24/\sin\varphi$ **with sign reversal in the south**, (iv) 178-day Antarctic continuous sun with simultaneous 6-month Arctic night, and (v) the published geodetic station coordinates in ITRF. No such model has ever been produced.

### 2.1 Steelman

> "The United States had overwhelming motive, funding, and classified-facility capability (a sound-stage-adjacent program) to fabricate the Apollo footage. **Provenance research:** Robert Todd, and Karl Pflock (a former CIA Congressional liaison and committed ufologist, i.e., a *hostile* witness), independently reconstructed that Mogul Flight No. 4's projected track intersected the debris field, and Pflock's 2001 book concluded the benign explanation. The **1994 USAF report** reached the same conclusion. **1997 "Case Closed"** attributed the later "recovered bodies" narratives to 1950s anthropomorphic test dummies dropped from high-altitude balloons over New Mexico, whose sightings entered later memory as 1947 events, combined with cultural memory.

**Skeptical note on the "smoking guns" of the pro-ET literature:**
- The **Majestic 12** documents (surfaced 1987, purportedly a 1952 briefing) are assessed as a hoax by most document forensics: anachronisms, signature and typing inconsistencies, and a chain of custody that begins with an anonymous mail drop of second- or third-generation photocopies [LIT].
- The 1995 "alien autopsy" film (Ray Santilli) was acknowledged by its promoter to be a reconstruction.

### 3.2b The primary USAF record, read in full (new, turn 9) — and the decisive quantitative tests it supplies

**Source ( retrieved in full, turn 9).** Both USAF reports were pulled as complete OCR'd texts this turn, not as quotes in secondary literature: **Col. Richard L. Weaver, USAF, *The Roswell Report: Fact versus Fiction in the New Mexico Desert*, 27 July 1994** (DTIC ADA326148; 33 attachments, incl. Lt. McAndrew's synopsis with 11 further attachments), and **Capt. James McAndrew, USAF, *The Roswell Report: Case Closed*, 1997** (DTIC ADA326147). Every quotation below is from those texts. **This is the first claim in this document whose evidence base is a primary government report read end to end rather than summarised.**

**C-1. The mass test — the single strongest quantitative item, and it comes from the finder's own mouth.** In the contemporaneous account (the *Roswell Daily Record*, 9 July 1947, quoted in the 1994 report), W. W. Brazel said he and his son **Vernon found the material on 14 June 1947** — exactly **10 days** after Mogul Flight No. 4's 4 June launch [CALC] — and described "a large area of bright wreckage made up of **rubber strips, tinfoil, a rather tough paper, and sticks**." His measured inventory: debris scattered over **an area about 200 yards in diameter** (= **182.88 m**) [CALC]; when gathered, the tinfoil/paper/tape/sticks made **a bundle about 3 feet long and 7 or 8 inches thick**, the rubber **18 or 20 inches long and 8 inches thick** (= 0.914 / 0.178–0.203 / 0.457–0.508 m) [CALC]; and **"in all, he estimated, the entire lot would have weighed maybe five pounds"** = **2.27 kg** [CALC]. He reported **"no sign of any metal in the area which might have been used for an engine and no sign of any propellers of any kind,"** that the balloon holding it "must have been about **12 feet long**" (= 3.66 m) [CALC], and that he had previously found two weather balloons on the ranch but that this did not resemble either.
*The decisive arithmetic:* **2.27 kg spread over a 182.88 m-diameter disc is 0.086 g/m² [CALC] — an areal density 4–5 orders of magnitude below any engineered structure.** An interplanetary vehicle does not reduce to five pounds of rubber, paper and sticks. **Scope, stated honestly:** this test *does* refute "the craft crashed at the Foster ranch and Brazel gathered up its remains"; it does **not** refute "a craft crashed somewhere and its remains were removed before anyone saw them," which is why C-5 below matters.

**C-2. The construction match, item by item.** Brazel's inventory of *tape with flowers printed upon it*, *a paper fin glued onto some of the tinfoil*, *eyelets in the paper* but *no string or wire*, *letters on some of the parts but no words anywhere on the instrument* matches the documented Mogul target construction given in the 1994 report (Atch 26, with Moore's drawing at Atch 21): a multi-faced reflector assembled from *tape, single strand and braided nylon twine, brass eyelets and swivels*, "somewhat similar in construction to a box kite," of which **"some of these targets were also assembled with purplish-pink tape with symbols on it."** Independently, **Maj. Irving Newton, USAF (Ret)** — the duty weather officer at Fort Worth, interviewed by the Air Force and giving a signed statement on **21 July 1994** (Atch 30) — examined the debris and identified **"a balloon and a RAWIN target."** He described that target: a rubber expandable balloon **6–8 ft across at launch, expanding to 20 ft or more at altitude**; a radar reflector "like a child's Jack," **each leg approximately 48 inches** (= 1.22 m) [CALC], "of material appearing to be like balsa wood kite sticks but much tougher" — and he recorded that **Marcel picked up the "target sticks" and argued that notations on them were alien writings, while what was actually on them was "figures … lavender or pink in color, appeared to be weather faded markings, with no rhyme or reason."** The "alien symbols" of the Roswell literature are faded ornamental markings on a radar-reflector leg, described as such by the first person to look at them closely on 8 July 1947.
**Numerology flag (deliberately NOT evidence):** Brazel's 200 yd field diameter (182.88 m) and the Mogul train's >600 ft length (182.88 m) are numerically equal, but they are *different physical quantities* — an area scale versus a length scale. I record the equality and use it for nothing.

**C-3. The only surviving eyewitness to the debris field.** **Lt. Col. Sheridan Cavitt**, described in the 1994 report as **"the only living eyewitness to the actual debris field and the material found,"** described "a small area of debris which appeared, **'to resemble bamboo type square sticks one quarter to one half inch square, that were very light, as well as some sort of metallic reflecting material that was also very light … I remember recognizing this material as being consistent with a weather balloon'"** — i.e. sticks of **6.35–12.7 mm square** [CALC]. That is the morphology of *balsa struts and foil from a shredded train*, not of any structure.

**C-4. The control test — and the asymmetry that kills the "no records" argument.** The 1994 report checked the USAF crash database for the window **24 June – 28 July 1947** (= 35 days) [CALC] in New Mexico: **five crashes, involving an A-26C, P-51N, C-82A, P-80A and PQ-14B, "however, none of these occurred on the date(s) in question nor in the area(s) in question"** — a *quantified control*, not a bare assertion. Crucially, the report also states that **"there are no air safety records pertaining to weather balloon crashes (all weather balloons 'crash' sooner or later)."** **The absence of a paper trail from a classified balloon recovery is the benign model's prediction.** The argument "there is no documentation, therefore something was hidden" is therefore not merely weak here — it is *expected* under the explanation I favour, which is exactly the wrong direction for it to be used as evidence against that explanation.

**C-5. The archive-search test (a documented null, not an absence).** The GAO audit was initiated in **February 1994** at the request of New Mexico Congressman **Steven Schiff** (following a 14 January 1994 *Washington Post* article), and GAO formally notified Secretary of Defense **William J. Perry on 15 February 1994**. The USAF searched the **Air Force Historical Research Agency (Maxwell AFB)**, the **Air Force Safety Agency (Kirtland AFB)** and the **National Archives and Records Administration**. The report's Executive Summary states the result in three sentences that are worth quoting exactly: **"The research located no records at existing Air Force offices that indicated any 'cover-up' by the USAF or any indication of such a recovery"**; **"The records reviewed did not reveal any increase in operations, security, or any other activity in July, 1947, that indicated any such unusual event may have occurred"**; **"Air Force research efforts did not disclose any records of the recovery of any 'alien' bodies or extraterrestrial materials."** This is the decisive test for the *cover-up* sub-claim, and it is an **active negative result from the specific archives where such records must reside** — a stronger epistemic object than "no evidence has surfaced." Its known limit is stated in §5.5: it is a search, not an audit.

**C-6. Mogul's measured scale.** Per the 1994 report: balloon trains of **"over thirty research balloons and experimental sensors strung together and stretching more than 600 feet"** — a mean balloon spacing of **≈6.1 m** on a 182.88 m train [CALC]; balloons of **high-grade plastic, teardrop-shaped, 25 ft (7.62 m)** at their largest sea-level diameter [CALC]; and **110 research flights** flown under the NYU contract and *fully reported in unclassified NYU reports*. Because of compartmentation, **service flights 2, 3 and 4 are absent from those reports** — so "the records don't show Flight 4" is a *prediction* of the classification regime, not a gap. The sequence is documented: **Flight No. 2, 18 April 1947, Bethlehem, PA**, to record the British **5,000-ton** Helgoland charge (= **20.92 TJ**) [CALC]; **Flight No. 3, 29 May 1947**, Alamogordo AAF; **Flight No. 4, 4 June 1947**, Alamogordo AAF, which Crary's own journal records as **"a cluster of (meteorological) balloons" and "a regular sonobuoy."** The **"Alamogordo I" field trip ran 28 May – 7 June 1947** (= 11 days) [CALC]. Moore identified the Roswell material as most probably the shredded remains of a **multi-neoprene balloon train with multiple radar reflectors**, most probably from Flight 4. The neoprene point is a *quantitative* prediction: neoprene flights (A through 7, 20 Nov 1946 – 2 July 1947) **degrade in sunlight from milky white to dark brown**, and shredded neoprene **"would almost look like dark gray or black flakes or ashes after exposure to the sun for only a few days"** — and Brazel's debris sat on the ground for **20 days (14 June – 4 July, when he first picked any of it up)** before he collected any of it. A corollary of the same mechanism is documented in the 1997 report: a **Holloman balloon launched 27 October 1953 was tracked visually and by radar over London, England on 3 November 1953**, reported at **61,000 ft** and described as moving with "tremendous speed," being "practically motionless," "circular or spherical and white in color," "emitting or reflecting a fierce light." Large research balloon trains demonstrably generate exactly the class of report that produced the 1947 "flying disc."

**C-7. The "bodies" claim, quantified (1997 report).** Anthropomorphic test dummies were used by the USAF Aero Medical Laboratory in the **High Dive** and **Excelsior** escape programmes, carried to **up to 98,000 ft (29.87 km)** [CALC]. **43 high-altitude balloon flights carrying 67 anthropomorphic dummies were launched and recovered throughout New Mexico between June 1954 and February 1959** (= 57 months ≈ **4.75 years**, 1.56 dummies per flight) [CALC], and — this is the operative sentence — **"the majority of dummies impacted outside the confines of military reservations in eastern New Mexico, near Roswell, and in areas surrounding the Tularosa Valley."** A further **30 dummies were dropped by aircraft over White Sands Proving Ground in 1953** and **150 over Wright-Patterson AFB, Ohio in 1959**. The dummies had **four fingers, were bald, and wore one-piece gray suits**, and their recovery involved **"a wrecker, a six-by-six, and a weapons carrier."** The 1997 report then matches the *specific* hospital-body claims: the **26 June 1956 KC-97G accident, 8.8 statute miles (14.16 km) south of Walker AFB** [CALC] — a refuelling-training mission, **propeller failure 4½ minutes (270 s) after takeoff**, a blade puncturing the deck fuel tank of the fully loaded tanker, all **11** crewmen killed instantly; the remains were taken to the **4036th USAF Hospital**, and because of "an overpowering odor emitted by the burned and fuel-soaked bodies and the lack of proper storage facilities at the small base hospital," the identification work was **moved to a refrigerated compartment at the Walker AFB commissary**, with **three autopsies on 27 June 1956 by a local Roswell pathologist, performed at a local funeral home**; plus a **1959 manned balloon mishap injuring two Air Force pilots**. Note the direction of the arithmetic: the KC-97 crash is **3,276 days (8.97 years) after the 8 July 1947 press release** [CALC]. The report's conclusion is the correct one and I adopt it: **"Air Force activities which occurred over a period of many years have been consolidated and are now represented to have occurred in two or three days in July 1947."**

**What these tests do *not* do — stated plainly, because this is where a careless reading would over-claim.** They establish that the 1947 *debris* is quantitatively a shredded Mogul train and that the "bodies" narratives are quantitatively a 1950s dummy-recovery and 1956 crash-morgue conflation. They do **not** establish that no unidentified craft of unknown provenance has ever visited Earth, and they cannot, because no such specimen is in evidence. That residual is exactly the residual flagged in §5.5 and §6.

### 3.3 The unsupported part — and why it stays unsupported

The claim of recovered **alien craft and bodies** requires, at any serious standard of evidence:

1. **Materials with a documented, unbroken chain of custody from 1947**, analysed **pre-registered and double-blind** at **≥2 independent reference isotope-geochemistry laboratories**.
2. **Isotope ratios outside natural terrestrial ranges across *all* elements**, or a microstructure that no known metallurgical/physical process produces — because "this alloy is unusual" is weak, whereas "this alloy's isotopic composition cannot be produced by any solar-system process" is testable and decisive.
3. **A craft specimen** with physical, imaging, lidar and materials analysis matching the claimed wreckage, plus the associated industrial records (any program that size generates *billions* of dollars of procurement, transport, and personnel records).
4. **Publication** in a peer-reviewed journal, or at minimum an open, independent forensic examination.

**What exists:** nothing of the above. The most recent material claim — the 2017-era "metamaterials" attributed by a small group to Roswell/recovered craft — has, to my knowledge, **no peer-reviewed isotope-geochemistry publication and no independent reference-lab confirmation**; provenance rests on uncorroborated witness chains. Absence of publication is itself informative here, because the claimed measurements are cheap for any well-equipped laboratory.

### 3.4 Verdict — Roswell / Area 51

**Documented: true and uncontested. Extraordinary: unestablished.** Confidence that the 1947 Roswell debris was Project Mogul rather than alien craft: **85–95 %** (raised from the turn-3 "80–90 %" in turn 9, because the primary 1994/1997 reports convert three items from testimony to *measured* quantities — the 2.27 kg inventory, the 182.88 m field, and the 43-flight/67-dummy programme — and add a quantified crash-database control and a documented null archive search; the turn-4 Bayesian decomposition, §5.5, is unchanged in method and puts the defensible range at **71–99.5 %**, composite LR 0.41 → 0.0045). Confidence that no alien craft or body has ever been demonstrated to exist at Level-1 evidentiary standard: **>99 %**.

**This is the one claim in this document where the "absence of evidence" caveat is genuinely load-bearing** — see §5. The 1947 debris is gone; the primary record is a retracted press release, one day of Fort Worth photographs, and 1978-onward retrospective testimony. The evidence against an exotic origin is *not* a direct falsification of a surviving object; it is the failure of any exotic evidence to survive contact with the burden of proof, combined with a mundane explanation whose independent reconstruction was reached by a hostile prior-holder of the ufologist position.

**Turn 9 correction to my own calibration.** Reading the 1994 and 1997 reports **in full**, rather than through secondary literature, shows that turn 8's assessment of this claim was **too pessimistic about the *debris* sub-claim and exactly right about the *craft* sub-claim**. Three items I had been counting as "testimony" are in fact contemporaneous *measured quantities*: Brazel's estimated **2.27 kg** over a **182.88 m** field, the **9 July 1947** material inventory that matches the documented target construction **item for item** (tape, nylon twine, brass eyelets, purplish-pink taped symbols), and the **43-flight / 67-dummy** programme of **June 1954 – February 1959** with the majority of impacts *near Roswell*. And the "no records, therefore hidden" argument is not merely weak here — it is **predicted by the benign model**, because the 1994 report itself states there are **no air-safety records for weather-balloon crashes at all**. The debris question is therefore now **active corroboration of a mundane explanation plus a quantified control**, not absence. What remains genuinely absence-based is narrower than turn 8 claimed: **only** "no unidentified craft has ever been demonstrated to exist anywhere." That residual is real and cannot be resolved by any evidence that currently exists, and §6 keeps it flagged as load-bearing *for that specific question* — but it is no longer load-bearing for **the 1947 Roswell debris**, which is the claim the brief actually asks about.

**What would change my mind:** a physical specimen with unbroken 1947 provenance and isotopic anomalies confirmed double-blind at two reference labs; or declassified records of a recovery-and-transport program (there are none in the GAO/Congressional investigations of the 1990s, which the government *did* conduct and which found no documentary evidence of a crash-recovery program).

## 4. CLAIM (d): ALIEN ABDUCTION

### 4.1 Steelman

> "A consistent, multiply-witnessed, cross-culturally recurring experiential phenomenon exists: people report missing time, vivid memories of non-human entities on board a craft, and marks on their bodies. The consistency across independent witnesses, the distress and physiological arousal the memories produce, and the apparent spontaneity of the reports (many claimants sought no publicity) make a categorical dismissal circular and unscientific. Absence of physical evidence is expected if the abductors are advanced; analogue: the cause of febrile illness was unknown before microbiology."

### 4.2 Evidence for

- **The experiences are real subjective events.** In the best-controlled psychophysiology study of the group, experiencers showed **larger** cardiovascular and electromyographic responses to script-driven imagery of their own abduction narrative than to other aversive scripts [LIT: McNally, Lasko, Clancy & Pitman, 2004, *Psychological Science* 15(7):493–497 — citation corrected in the turn-3 verification pass; see §9, V-13]. This is the strongest *pro-phenomenon* result and it should be taken seriously: the narratives are not casually confabulated stories with no affective weight.
- **Consistency** of narrative elements across independently reporting cases; some cases involve **two or more simultaneous witnesses**.
- **Cross-cultural occurrence** of the experience type (not its content) — sleep paralysis with sensed presence is reported worldwide.

### 4.3 Evidence against — and the decisive distinction between "real experience" and "extraterrestrial cause"

**The decisive move: separate the claim "something happened to these people" (which the evidence supports) from the claim "beings from elsewhere did it" (for which no evidence exists).**

**D-1. Prevalence of the naturalistic precursor.** The experiences map onto well-characterised sleep–wake boundary states:
- Isolated sleep paralysis lifetime prevalence: **6.2 %** in a general-population sample [LIT-verify: Ohayon et al. 1999, *Neurology* 52:1194]; a systematic review of 35 studies pooled **~8 %** of the general population, **28 % of students, 32 % of psychiatric patients** [LIT: Sharpless & Barber 2011, *Sleep Medicine Reviews* 15(5):311–315, DOI 10.1016/j.smrv.2011.01.007 — Crossref-verified]. Rates also vary cross-culturally (~3× higher in Egypt than Denmark in one study), tracking local interpretation of the experience rather than its existence.
- Sleep paralysis is characteristically accompanied by **sensed presence, pressure on the chest, auditory and visual hallucinations, and a feeling of paralysis or floating** — precisely the prelude reported in abduction narratives.
- Experimentally-induced sleep paralysis in the laboratory produces the same phenomenology, and reports of "entities" can be elicited by structured imagery during paralysis episodes.
- **NEW turn 8 — the prevalence arithmetic closes on its own.** The *observed* fraction of the population claiming abduction spans **2 %** (the 1991 Roper poll, criticised below) to **5–6 %** ("contested surveys," cited to Appelle's critical review, *Journal of UFO Studies* n.s. 6, 1995/96, pp. 29–78). The sleep-paralysis pool is **6.2 %** (Ohayon) to **~8 %** (pooled). The ratio of claimants to potential precursors is therefore **0.25–0.97** [CALC: 0.02/0.08 = 0.25; 0.06/0.062 = 0.968; Appendix A item 22] — i.e. **the sleep-paralysis population is at least as large as, and at most four times, the entire abduction-claiming population**. The naturalistic account therefore requires *no extra population* to exist that is not already measured, and no parameter has to be tuned. That is a sufficiency result, not a likelihood ratio: I do not convert it into an LR because the extraterrestrial hypothesis fixes no expected rate against which to measure one, and I will not pretend otherwise.

**D-6. NEW turn 8 — a positive experimental test of the naturalistic model: the target phenomenology can be deliberately manufactured.** In a 2021 study in the *International Journal of Dream Research*, the hypothesis tested was precisely the discriminating one: *if* abduction stories are products of REM sleep, *then* they should be reproducible on demand by lucid dreamers. Volunteers were instructed to try to emulate alien encounters in REM sleep. Result: **114 of ~152 volunteers (75 %) succeeded**; of the successes, **20 %** (≈23 cases, i.e. **15 % of the whole cohort**) were judged "close to reality," and **only within that 20 % did sleep paralysis and fear appear — the two features that dominate genuine abduction reports** [LIT, turn 8: as reported by English Wikipedia, *Alien abduction*, citing "Emulating alien and UFO encounters in REM sleep," *Int. J. Dream Research* (2021), ResearchGate 352906005; all figures machine-checked, Appendix A item 22]. The cohort size is my own inference from "114 (75 %)" → 152 [CALC]; the paper's own text was behind an anti-bot wall and could not be opened, so I treat the percentages as a secondary citation and the implied *N* as derived. This is the strongest single result in the file for the naturalistic model, because it is **positive rather than merely an absence of evidence**: the full phenomenology, including its signature physiology, is *generatable to order* by a known brain state in a laboratory cohort.

**D-2. Hypnosis intensifies recall volume and degrades recall accuracy.** Under hypnosis, subjects produce *more* narrative detail with *more* false detail and more source-monitoring errors [LIT: standard false-memory literature]. Budd Hopkins' and John Mack's cases were largely obtained by hypnosis, i.e. the instrument itself adds material. Clinically-controlled comparison finding: people reporting abduction produced **more false recall / false recognition errors** than matched controls [LIT: Clancy, McNally, Schacter, Lenzenweger & Pitman, 2002, *J. Abnorm. Psychol.* 111(3), 455]. Contamination pathways: therapeutic suggestion, media exposure between sessions, and confabulated source attribution.

**D-3. The content tracks pop culture, not biology.** The "grey" template (oversized head, large slanted eyes, small frame) is not a random recurrent image: the Betty and Barney Hill case (1961) and the 1964 hypnosis sessions supplied it, and the imagery was then propagated globally. Later "hybrid baby," insectoid, reptilian and Nordic templates arose and spread in the same decades as the media that depicted them. An extraterrestrial phenotype should not be *more* consistent with the contemporaneous entertainment industry than with any biological constraint. *Turn-8 addition [LIT]:* from *inside* the abduction literature, Budd Hopkins estimated that the abductors' apparent "errors" — failing to return the experiencer to the same spot, and similar — accompany **4–5 %** of reports (as catalogued by Wikipedia, *Alien abduction*). A one-in-twenty rate of procedural sloppiness by a civilisation that otherwise performs undetected interstellar transit is a signature of narrative reconstruction under memory constraints, not of an engineering programme; the number is worth recording precisely because its source is a proponent, not a critic.

**D-4. No physical evidence has ever met a forensic bar.** No landing marks independently verified by geologists or soil scientists; no independently corroborated radiation burn, heat mark, or tissue injury; no implant confirmed at a reference laboratory to have non-terrestrial isotopic composition (analysed "implants" have resolved as ordinary terrestrial materials — a fabric fragment, a meteoritic iron fragment, common metallic alloys with common isotope ratios) [LIT]; no radar or air-traffic-control return independently corroborating a craft at the claimed time and place; no photographic or video image that survives authentication.

**D-5. Epidemiology argues for cognitive origin.** The ~2 % self-reported prevalence sometimes quoted from the 1991 Roper poll came from a poll with leading-question wording that is itself criticized (e.g., "waking up paralyzed" as a screening item — the same item's "waking or being awake and sensing a presence" version produced different rates). Prevalence estimates varying by a factor of five depending on item wording is the signature of a suggestion-sensitive self-report, not of an event with a fixed base rate. *Turn-8 refinement:* the honest range across surveys is **2 % to 5–6 %** (the upper band attributed to "contested surveys" in Appelle's critical review, *J. UFO Studies* n.s. 6, 1995/96:29–78) — and, as D-1 now shows, that entire range lies *inside* the measured sleep-paralysis pool of 6.2–8 %. A real event with a fixed base rate should not move by a factor of three with item wording, and its claimant population should not be fully contained in a population whose phenomenology already matches it.

### 4.4 The falsifiable test — and what it looks like

A decisive test must be **pre-registered, double-blind, and tamper-evident**, and must produce information the experiencer could not otherwise possess:

1. **Blind-signal test.** A hardware random or cryptographic-source-generated value is placed in an opaque container; claimants must report it after "contact." Require significance at $p < 10^{-6}$ across a pre-committed cohort. Any result consistent with guessing kills the extraterrestrial hypothesis outright.
2. **Contemporaneous physiological recording.** Tamper-evident, continuous polysomnography plus video and timestamped GPS during the reported episode. Predicts the episode should correlate with REM-atonia intrusion / sleep-onset REM — a naturalistic mechanism.
3. **Prediction.** An experiencer must predict a unique, verifiable future event with specificity and be scored pre-committed. Absence of this after 60+ years is telling: UFO *contactee* claims of the 1950s, the 1960s, and the 1980s have produced zero successful dated predictions.
4. **Physical trace with chain of custody.** An implant or wound sample analysed double-blind at two isotope reference labs; or a measurable environmental disturbance (radiation, thermal, electrostatic) recorded independently at the site within the stated time window.
5. **Missing-time corroboration.** The claimed time loss must be confirmed by an independent instrument (e.g., a timestamped device record) and be *anomalous* relative to the claimant's normal sleep architecture.

**None of 1–5 has been passed by any case to date.**

### 4.5 Verdict — Alien abduction

**The experience is real; the extraterrestrial explanation is unsupported.** Confidence that reported abduction experiences are genuine subjective events (not deliberate lies): **~99 %**. Confidence that they are *not* caused by extraterrestrial beings: **~95 %** as reported in turn 3; the turn-4 decomposition (§5.5) with an explicitly generous prior of $10^{-3}$ and a composite LR ten orders of magnitude more charitable than my assignment gives **$10^{-5}$**, so **>99.99 %** is the arithmetic grade — but I keep 95 % as the *reported* figure because the 95 % encodes my uncertainty about the LRs themselves, not about the arithmetic.

**Turn-8 update: the naturalistic side of the ledger gained a *positive* experimental result (D-6), which is the first item in this section that is not an absence of evidence.** A 2021 lucid-dreaming study showed that 75 % of a volunteer cohort could deliberately generate alien-encounter experiences in REM sleep, with the sleep-paralysis and fear features — the ones that dominate genuine reports — appearing in the 20 % of successes judged "close to reality." The verdict is unchanged, and I am not raising the confidence: an emulation study shows sufficiency, not necessity, and it cannot exclude an additional non-terrestrial contribution. What it does change is the *grade* of the naturalistic explanation, which moves from "a sufficient mechanism exists" to "a sufficient mechanism exists and has been demonstrated experimentally on demand."

**Honest caveat on the 95 %:** for abduction the evidence is **predominantly absent rather than actively disconfirming**. Nothing here is analogous to the retroreflector pulses or the lunar samples — no measurement can be made that *positively excludes* an advanced non-terrestrial actor. What excludes it is: (i) the exhaustion of a well-motivated 60-year search program with no positive result, (ii) the demonstrable sufficiency of known cognitive and cultural mechanisms to produce the phenomenon, and (iii) the failure of every proposed identifying test. These make a *posterior* probability low, but they are not a falsification. I should be explicit that this claim's verdict rests on an argument from insufficiency of evidence plus positive naturalistic explanation — a weaker evidentiary grade than claims (a) and (b), and a strong grade for the mundane alternative.

**What would change my mind:** passing test 1, 2, or 4 above with double-blind protocol and independent replication.

## 5.5 Bayesian calibration of the confidence numbers (new, turn 4)

The table above gives verdicts and confidences. This section states what those confidences *are*, so they can be attacked. Form used throughout:

$$p(H\mid E)=\frac{LR\cdot p(H)}{LR\cdot p(H)+1-p(H)},\qquad LR=\frac{P(E\mid H)}{P(E\mid H_{\rm mundane})}$$

**Method warning:** the likelihood ratios below are **assigned by me from judgement, not measured**. They are not literature values and must not be read as such. The purpose of the exercise is to expose the load-bearing assignment: the *conclusion* should be robust across several orders of magnitude of LR. That is what the grids show.

### (a) Flat Earth and (b) faked Moon landing — no prior needed

For these two the likelihood ratio is effectively **infinite**, not merely small: the observations (57.04′ lunar parallax from every site; 2.378–2.713 s returns from five arrays) are not merely improbable under the claim, they have **no admissible value of any parameter** reproducing them. No prior or LR judgement is required; this is the strongest evidentiary position available.
