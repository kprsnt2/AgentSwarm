=== H. SUMMARY OF COMPUTED RESULTS ===
H1  Per-case LR for a Stevenson-style case at S=10, K=100 ranges from 1e28 (fully
    blind, sigma=1e-3) down to 1e-2 (b=0.2, guided elicitation, sigma=1e-1): a
    30-order-of-magnitude swing carried entirely by two unmeasured protocol
    parameters. No existing corpus measures either one.
H2  Corpus arithmetic is never binding: at any per-case LR >= 10^0.5, n=2500 cases
    gives a corpus BF beyond 10^1000. The numerology is not the weak point.
H3  The weak point is measurement, not strength: estimating the naturalistic
    false-match rate p=1e-3 to +/-25% needs ~6.1e4 blinded match attempts
    (~6.1e3 blinded case-verifications). No such measurement exists; the corpus is
    therefore UNSCORABLE rather than weak.
H4  Cultural clustering is knife-edge ambiguous under belief-independent rebirth
    (R=100: ambiguous only for rho in (20.8,22.0)) and is predicted by BOTH readings
    under near-home rebinding. Its force depends on which rebirth prediction is
    stipulated, so it is a contested candidate, not a discriminator.
H5  A 1e6 corpus BF is fully consumed by three kappa=1e-2 auxiliaries; the tax is
    symmetric across all cores.
H6  Even a confirmed corpus re-runs attribution invariance inside the rebirth-accepting
    family: max drift from priors = 0.000000 bits at BF=1e6.
H7  Prior-lock: a core can absorb a 1e12 corpus with ~1e8:1 prior odds plus two
    auxiliaries.

All quantities describe evidential structure. No probability of any theological
proposition is asserted, and no verdict is expressed.
