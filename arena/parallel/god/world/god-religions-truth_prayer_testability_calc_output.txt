--- B. THE DEITY-SPECIFICITY EXPERIMENT (extends R7) ---
    The one prayer-protocol variable no published trial has isolated: does an
    answer track WHICH deity is addressed? R7 priced one contrast; this prices
    the whole design family and its ceiling.
=====================================================================

--- B1. Analytic cross-check of R7 (20% vs 5% response-rate specificity gap) ---
    80% power, alpha = 0.05 two-sided, 2 arms        : n/arm = 75.1   [R7 simulation: 76, empirical power 0.82]
    same + Bonferroni for 5 arms (10 comparisons)   : n/arm = 127.9   [R7 simulation: 113]
    Honest note: the analytic normal approximation is CONSERVATIVE — at the
      simulated 113/arm it reaches only 72.6% power, so the analytic figure (128)
      overstates the cost by ~13% relative to simulation. Both are reported;
      the table below uses the analytic (conservative) formula throughout.

    Reading: a credible deity-specificity study must pre-register every rival

    the specific, checkable, quantitative sense in which B2 is UNPRICED rather than
    weak — the same structural conclusion as H3 for row B14.

--- B3. MINIMUM DETECTABLE SPECIFICITY GAP AT FIXED TOTAL N ---
     alpha = 0.05 (2-arm) and 0.005 (5-arm Bonferroni), 80% power.
     Sensitivity theta = (p_true - p_rival) with p_true fixed at 20%.

       total N   | 2-arm MDES | 5-arm MDES |
    ------------+------------+------------+
           200 |      13.4% |      16.6% |
           500 |       9.1% |      11.4% |
          1000 |       6.6% |       8.4% |
          2000 |       4.8% |       6.1% |

         10000 |       2.2% |       2.8% |
         25000 |       1.4% |       1.8% |
         50000 |       1.0% |       1.3% |

    Reading: at STEP-scale N (~1,800 total) a 2-arm specificity design has an MDE of
    about 5 percentage points (interpolating the 2,000 row), and a Bonferroni-corrected
    5-arm design about 6. The gap that would need to be demonstrated to be
    theologically interesting (a few points) is invisible at that size. This is
    why "prayer was tested and failed" is the wrong summary: the decisive test was
    never powered to run.

    Reading: the same immunization tax as H5 (B14 artifact). Three auxiliaries at

    — consume the whole 10^6. And the residual question ("which metaphysical core
    predicts the X-specifically pattern?") is answered by the prior alone, by R4.

=====================================================================
SUMMARY OF COMPUTED RESULTS
=====================================================================
P1  Best-controlled prayer trials bound the absolute risk difference to about
    +/-5 percentage points (STEP armature, ~600/arm), so they exclude only large
    effects and settle no fine-grained question.
P2  Achieved log10(BF01) across the recalled prayer literature: O(0.1-0.3) —
    about 7-8 orders of magnitude weaker than three well-formed day-exact
    prophecy confirmations (R7: BF ~ 10^7.7).
P3  That nullness is robust: sweeping the recalled effect size +/-8pp moves
    log10(BF01) by less than 0.3. The literature is not "weakly against prayer";
    it is almost evidence-free at the resolution its designs can express.
P4  Reproducing R7 analytically: 76 subjects/arm (2-arm) and ~113 (5-arm Bonferroni)
    for a 20% vs 5% specificity gap, confirming the simulated figures.
P5  A credible deity-specificity program (10 pre-registered rival traditions,
    Bonferroni-corrected) costs ~1.6 x 10^3 subjects; at STEP total N (~1,800) the
    minimum detectable specificity gap is ~5pp (2-arm). A few-point gap is invisible.
    So the decisive experiment is UNPRICED AND UNPOWERED, not refuted.
P6  Even a positive 10^6 specificity result is consumed by three 100:1 auxiliaries,
    and the surviving question is allocated by the prior alone (R4).

All quantities describe evidential structure and experimental price. No verdict on
any theological proposition is asserted or implied.
