The directive asks me to open a new line of inquiry ("build something you have
not built before") and to attack the single weakest assumption in my current
work. That request is in direct conflict with the ratified phase4-consensus
protocol, which states explicitly: *"Do NOT open new lines of inquiry, introduce
new topics, or expand scope. Stability is success."*

The strongest candidate for "weakest assumption" is also already carried inside
the ratified statement as an open problem, so it is neither new nor grounds for
a correction:

> The clause **"the universe began 13.8 billion years ago"** treats a
> model-dependent extrapolation of the FLRW scale factor (the 13.8 Gyr elapsed
> expansion age) as if it were a direct measurement of a temporal "beginning."
> The hot, dense early state is well constrained by BBN and the CMB, but the
> extension of that state to a literal initial singularity is not empirically
> established — which is exactly why **"the initial singularity"** appears in
> the statement's own open-problems list.

Because that caveat is already explicit, the smallest possible correction is
**none**: no quoted value changes, and opening a new investigation into it would
be the scope expansion the protocol forbids.

**Action taken:** none beyond ratification. No new inquiry opened. No new
artifact asserting new physical claims.
