### 4.2 The Carvaka / Lokayata Critique of Metaphysical Inference

The Carvaka school advanced a radical empirical critique:
- **Refutation of *Anumana* for Unobservables:** Invariable concomitance (*Vyapti*, e.g., "wherever there is smoke, there is fire") can only be established for observed instances. ### 4.3 Dignaga and Dharmakirti’s Buddhist Dismantling of Theism

Buddhist logicians (Dignaga, Dharmakirti in *Pramanavarttika*) systematically dismantled the Nyaya cosmological argument for a creator God:
- The Nyaya school argued: "The universe, mountains, and rivers are effects; like pots, they must have an intelligent maker (*Ishvara*)."
- Dharmakirti demonstrated this argument commits the fallacy of **unestablished analogy (*Asiddha*)**: A pot is produced by a visible, embodied human artisan using physical tools in time. Transferring this observation to infer an unembodied, omniscient, eternal creator of mountains and oceans is an illicit inductive leap. - **Epistemic Finding:** Within orthodox (*Astika*) Hindu thought itself, reverence for sacred texts was formally severed from belief in an omnipotent creator God. Theism and scriptural tradition are structurally independent.

## 5. The Structural Mechanics of Why Metaphysical Claims Resist Empirical Testing

Why do Class B claims resist empirical adjudication? This resistance is not a contingent limitation of current instrumentation; it is a structural property of the propositions themselves.

### 5.1 Category Mismatch of Physical Instrumentation

Physical instruments register detection events via exchange of mass-energy or momentum:
$$\Delta p \cdot \Delta x \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}$$
Interactions must be mediated by the four fundamental forces: electromagnetic, strong nuclear, weak nuclear, or gravitational.
In contrast, classical Dharmic metaphysics defines *Brahman* and *Atman* as:
- *Atindriya:* Beyond sensory organs and physical coupling.
- *Nirguna:* Possessing zero physical attributes, charges, or masses.
- *Avyakta:* Unmanifest subjective observer rather than an observed object.

An entity defined as lacking physical coupling cannot exchange quanta with a detector. Physical non-detection cannot serve as evidence of absence.

### 5.2 The Bayesian Likelihood Invariance Theorem

Let $D \in \mathcal{D}$ represent any possible empirical dataset regarding the physical universe (e.g., fundamental constants, cosmic microwave background, DNA structure, fossil records, thermodynamic entropy, or distribution of human suffering).

Let $H_0$ be the physicalist-naturalist hypothesis (spacetime and mass-energy fields are ontological primitives).  
Let $H_1$ be the metaphysical theism/Brahman hypothesis (ultimate consciousness manifests the universe).

Because the believer model interprets every discovery of physical order, constant fine-tuning, or mathematical law as the manifestation of divine will (*Ishvara / Leela / Rta / Vyavaharika Satya*), both models assign identical likelihoods to the empirical observations:
$$P(D \mid H_1) = P(D \mid H_0)$$

The Likelihood Ratio $\Lambda(D)$ is:
$$\Lambda(D) \equiv \frac{P(D \mid H_1)}{P(D \mid H_0)} = 1.0000$$
The Log-Bayes Factor is:
$$\ln(\text{BF}) = \ln(1.0) = 0$$
The Information Gain (Kullback-Leibler divergence) is:
$$D_{\text{KL}}(P(D \mid H_1) \parallel P(D \mid H_0)) = \int P(D \mid H_1) \ln\left(\frac{P(D \mid H_1)}{P(D \mid H_0)}\right) dD = 0\text{ bits}$$

Applying Bayes' Theorem:
$$\frac{P(H_1 \mid D)}{P(H_0 \mid D)} = \frac{P(H_1)}{P(H_0)} \times \Lambda(D) = \frac{P(H_1)}{P(H_0)}$$

**Mathematical Conclusion:** The posterior odds ratio is identically equal to the prior odds ratio. No empirical observation in physical spacetime can alter the relative probability of the two hypotheses.
