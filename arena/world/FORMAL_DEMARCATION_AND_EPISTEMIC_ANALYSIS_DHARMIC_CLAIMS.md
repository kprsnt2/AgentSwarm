## 2. Established Ground Truths and Quantitative Baselines

### 2.1 Chronological and Philological Stratification of Texts

The Vedic and Upanishadic corpora are datable historical objects situated in South Asian antiquity:
- **Rigveda Samhita:** The oldest preserved text in the Indo-Aryan family, composed c. 1500–1200 BCE in the Sapta Sindhu (Punjab/Northwest). Dated via comparative Indo-Iranian linguistics (direct cognates with Old Avestan *Gathas*), internal metrical stratigraphy (archaic Family Books 2–7 vs later Books 1 and 10), and external synchronisms (the Mitanni-Hittite treaty of c. 1380 BCE invoking the Vedic deities *Mitra*, *Varuna*, *Indra*, and *Nasatya*).
- **Later Samhitas and Brahmanas:** Samaveda, Yajurveda, Atharvaveda, and associated prose liturgical commentaries (c. 1200–800 BCE). Geographically situated in the Kuru-Panchala realm, coinciding with the archaeological Painted Grey Ware (PGW) horizon and the transition from Late Bronze to Iron Age metallurgy.
- **Principal Upanishads:** (*Brihadaranyaka*, *Chandogya*, *Katha*, *Taittiriya*, *Isha*, *Kena*), dated to c. 800–500 BCE. They mark a syntactic transition toward early Classical Sanskrit and document an intellectual movement away from ritualistic sacrificial efficacy (*Karmakanda*) toward introspective ontological inquiry (*Jnanakanda*), preceding early Buddhism (Pali Canon) and Jainism.
- **Early Epigraphic Attestation of Bhagavata Deva Worship:** The Heliodorus Pillar inscription at Besnagar (Vidisha, Madhya Pradesh), dated securely to c. 113 BCE, records the erection of a Garuda pillar in honor of *Vasudeva* ("God of Gods", *Devadeva*) by Heliodorus, an ambassador of Indo-Greek king Antialcidas to King Bhagabhadra. This provides empirical proof of institutionalized devotional deity worship in North-Central India by the 2nd century BCE.

## 5. Epistemic Mechanics of Resistance: Why Class B Resists Adjudication

Why are Class B claims resistant to scientific testing? This resistance is not a contingent gap in current scientific instruments; it is an intrinsic structural property of the claims.

### 5.1 Radical Duhem-Quine Underdetermination & Bayesian Likelihood Invariance

In Bayesian epistemology, evidence $E$ can update the probability of hypothesis $H_1$ relative to $H_2$ if and only if the **Bayes Factor** ($\text{BF}$) departs from unity:
$$\text{BF} = \frac{P(E \mid H_1)}{P(E \mid H_2)} \ne 1.0 \implies \ln(\text{BF}) \ne 0$$

Let $E$ be any empirical physical observation: the existence of mathematical laws, fine-tuned constants, thermodynamic entropy, or biological evolution.
- Under **$H_{\text{Naturalism}}$** (physical fields are primary), $P(E \mid H_{\text{Naturalism}}) = 1.0$, as physical models are formulated to describe $E$.
- Under **$H_{\text{Brahman}}$** (Advaita Vedanta), the phenomenal universe (*Vyavaharika Satya*) is an emanation of *Brahman* through *Maya*. Any universe—whether ordered, chaotic, multi-dimensional, or flat—is equally consistent with *Brahman* as the substratum. Thus, $P(E \mid H_{\text{Brahman}}) = 1.0$.

$$\text{BF} = \frac{1.0}{1.0} = 1.0 \implies \ln(\text{BF}) = 0 \text{ bits of discriminative information}$$

Because the likelihood ratio is identically $1.0$, **no empirical measurement can increase or decrease the relative posterior probability of the metaphysical hypothesis**. The posterior is entirely dominated by the prior, which rests on non-empirical axiomatic commitments.

### 5.2 Category Mismatch of Physical Instrumentation

All empirical detection relies on standard quantum and relativistic force exchanges:
$$\Delta p \cdot \Delta x \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}, \quad \Delta E = \hbar \omega$$
An instrument registers an event if and only if there is a transfer of energy, momentum, or charge.
- Classical Dharmic philosophy explicitly defines *Brahman* and *Atman* as:
  - *Atindriya:* Beyond the grasp of sensory and physical instruments.
  - *Nirguna:* Possessing no physical mass, charge, color, spin, or spatio-temporal boundary.
  - *Drashta (The Seer):* The subjective ground of awareness, never an objective entity in spacetime.
- Attempting to detect *Brahman* with a particle collider or radio telescope is a fundamental **category error**—analogous to attempting to measure the political orientation of a photon or weighing the number 7 with a spring scale.

### 5.3 Causal Insulation and Retroactive Adjustability (The Karma Ledger)

For a causal law to be scientifically investigable, it must specify:
1. An identifiable physical carrier mechanism;
2. Observable, bounded latency between cause and effect;

- The carrier mechanism is posited as *Adrishta* (unseen force) or *Apurva* (trans-empirical potency), stored in a non-physical subtle body (*Sukshma Sharira*).
- The temporal delay between action (*Karma*) and result (*Phala*) spans indefinite trans-migratory lifetimes.
- The master ledger of accumulated actions (*Sanchita Karma*) is inaccessible to pre-event observational audit.

4. **Carvaka (Lokayata):**
   The materialist school restricted valid knowledge strictly to *Pratyaksha* (perception), rejecting *Sabda* and metaphysical *Anumana*. They argued that projecting unseen realms, devas, or karma from physical observations was an unwarranted leap.

**Epistemic Invariant:** The classical Indian tradition itself established that metaphysical claims cannot be extracted from empirical observation alone without accepting the autonomous authority of *Sruti*.
