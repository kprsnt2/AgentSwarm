- We do **not** claim that ancient Indian sages measured cosmic inflation or Hilbert-space wavefunctions.
- When calculating the $7,560\text{ light-year}$ sheath radius, we do not claim that the Bhagavata Purana discovered the interstellar medium; we calculate what the text's own stated units ($50\text{ crore yojanas} \times 10^k$) equal in modern units, demonstrating the internal logic of the ancient author's imagination.

### Adherence to Protocol Safeguard 2: Never Treating Absence of Evidence as Proof of Falsehood
- **Verification:** We do **not** commit the reductionist fallacy of dismissing the Hindu multiverse as "non-existent" simply because ancient authors lacked differential geometry or radio telescopes.
- The historical evidence is undeniable: classical Indian civilization was the **first in human history to formulate a boundless, cyclic, multi-universe architecture** spanning trillions of years, at a time when European thought was confined to a single, static, 6,000-year Ptolemaic-biblical sphere.

## 8. Established Ground Truths, Known Unknowns, and Falsification Conditions

### 8.1 Established Ground Truths
1. **The Concentric Sheath Architecture:** The Puranic *Brahmāṇḍa* is structurally composed of a planetary inner core ($43\text{ AU}$) surrounded by seven exponential elemental sheaths ($10^k$), expanding the complete envelope to **$7,560.4\text{ light-years}$ in radius** ($15,120\text{ light-years}$ in diameter).
2. **Cosmological Time Dilation Hierarchy:** Classical Puranic narratives explicitly document extreme relativistic-scale time dilation across ontological realms ($\Gamma_{\text{Kakudmī}} \approx 1.28 \times 10^{12}$).
3. **Multiverse Heterogeneity:** Classical texts explicitly document an ensemble of universes with heterogeneous dimensions and multi-headed Brahmas ($4, 8, 16, \dots, 1024\text{ heads}$).
4. **Epistemic Independence of Indian Astronomy:** Classical Indian astronomers (*Jyotiṣis*) explicitly operated with a single spherical terrestrial cosmos, rejecting Puranic multiple Brahmāṇḍas as non-empirical theology.

### 8.2 Known Unknowns
1. **Cross-Subcontinental Transmission Vectors:** The precise historical vectors of cross-pollination between Buddhist Mahāyāna Trichiliocosm models (dating from the 1st c. BCE) and Puranic *aneka-brahmāṇḍa* redactions (dating from 300–800 CE) remain partially untraced due to the loss of early north-Indian manuscripts.
2. **Numerological Derivation of the 500-Million Yojana Baseline:** It remains unknown whether the 50-crore yojana figure originated as an empirical extrapolation of solar angular diameter and daily velocity, or as a sacred numerological symmetry based on the sexagesimal system.

### 8.3 Conditions that Would Falsify or Revise this Assessment
1. **Discovery of Pre-Buddhist Vedic Texts with Explicit Multiple Brahmāṇḍas:**
   If an indisputably authentic early Vedic text (c. 1500–1000 BCE) is discovered containing the *aneka-brahmāṇḍa* doctrine, the established evolutionary chronology (Tri-loka $\rightarrow$ Aneka-Brahmāṇḍa) would be falsified.
2. **Discovery of Astronomical Observational Records for the Sheaths:**
   If a manuscript is found showing that the seven sheaths were derived from observational instruments (e.g., optical parallax or atmospheric refraction) rather than Sāṅkhya element metaphysics, the classification of the sheaths as metaphysical would be revised.
