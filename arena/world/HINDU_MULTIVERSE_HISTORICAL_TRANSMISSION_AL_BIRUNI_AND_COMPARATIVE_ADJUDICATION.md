## 2. Al-Bīrūnī's 11th-Century Epistemological Audit (*Indica* Ch. 20–28)

In Chapters 20 through 28 of *Kitāb fī Taḥqīq mā li-l-Hind*, Al-Bīrūnī undertakes a systematic comparative evaluation of Indian cosmography:

### 2.1. On the Brahmāṇḍa and Seven Sheaths (Chapter 20)
Al-Bīrūnī directly translates excerpts from the *Matsya Purāṇa*, *Vāyu Purāṇa*, and *Viṣṇu Purāṇa* regarding the origin of the Brahmāṇḍa:
> *"The egg was produced by the primary cause... surrounded by water, water by fire, fire by wind, wind by ether, ether by the origin of the elements, this by the intellect, and the intellect by the unmanifest... each sheath ten times as large as the preceding."* (Sachau Vol. I, Chap. 20)

Al-Bīrūnī notes that while this hierarchy mirrors the philosophical succession of elements in Sāṅkhya philosophy and Greek celestial spheres, it cannot be taken as physical astronomical geometry:
> *"If they mean by these sheaths physical, spherical bodies surrounding each other, the dimensions given by them are mathematically absurd and contradict the observed motions and parallax of the heavenly bodies."*

### 2.2. The Double Truth and the "Brahmagupta Compromise" (Chapter 21)
Al-Bīrūnī makes a profound sociological and epistemic distinction between two tiers of Indian intellectual life:
* **The Khawāṣṣ (The Intellectual / Scientific Elite):** Mathematical astronomers (*Jyotiṣa Siddhāntins*) like Āryabhaṭa, Varāhamihira, and Brahmagupta, who possessed advanced knowledge of spherical trigonometry, calculated planetary epicycles, knew the Earth is a sphere suspended freely in space, and understood eclipses as shadow phenomena.
* **The 'Awāmm (The Common Masses and Priests):** Adherents of literalist Puranic mythology who believed the Earth is a flat disc 500 million yojanas wide, that Mount Meru reaches 84,000 yojanas high, and that eclipses are caused by the demon Rāhu swallowing the Sun and Moon.

Al-Bīrūnī famously exposes how Brahmagupta capitulated to social pressure in *Brāhmasphuṭasiddhānta* (Chapter 21, *Gola-adhyāya*):
> *"Brahmagupta is one of the most distinguished of their astronomers... yet when he speaks of the eclipse, he says: 'Some people think that the eclipse is not produced by the Head (Rāhu). This is a foolish opinion, for the Veda, which is the word of God, says that the Head produces the eclipse... Therefore, we must follow the sacred text.' But look at what he does in his calculations! In the computational chapters he calculates the eclipse strictly by the diameter of the shadow and the lunar latitude, without mentioning Rāhu at all! He only lashed out at Āryabhaṭa to protect himself from the accusations of the priests."* (Sachau Vol. II, Chap. 59)
