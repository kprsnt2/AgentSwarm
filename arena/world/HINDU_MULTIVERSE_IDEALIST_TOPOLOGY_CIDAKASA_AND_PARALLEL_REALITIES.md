- In this trance, Lavaṇa experiences being thrown from a galloping horse in a terrifying desert, starving, being rescued by a dark-skinned tribal Pulinda maiden, living 60 years in a poverty-stricken outcaste village (*pukkasa-grāma*), fathering multiple children, suffering a devastating famine, watching his children cry for food, and finally leaping into a funeral pyre.
- Upon touching the flames, he snaps awake on his golden throne, surrounded by his courtiers, who confirm that only 48 minutes elapsed!

$$\gamma_{\text{Lavaṇa}} = \frac{60 \times 365.25 \times 86,400\text{ s}}{2,880\text{ s}} = \frac{1,893,456,000}{2,880} = \mathbf{657,450.0}$$

**The Paradox:** Months later, curious and disturbed, King Lavaṇa leads his royal cavalry south into the Vindhya forests. To his utter shock, he finds the **exact physical village**, meets the **exact tribal woman**, and hears the villagers lamenting the death of the stranger who jumped into the pyre!

### 4.2 Sage Gādhi’s Thirty-Second River Immersion
In *Yoga-Vāsiṣṭha* (Upāśama Prakaraṇa 44–49), the sage Gādhi enters the Gaṅgā to perform his morning water rituals (*sandhyā*). He dips his head under the water for **30 seconds**:

$$\gamma_{\text{Gādhi}} = \frac{60 \times 365.25 \times 86,400\text{ s}}{30.0\text{ s}} = \frac{1,893,456,000}{30.0} = \mathbf{63,115,200.0} \approx \mathbf{6.31 \times 10^7}$$

**The Paradox:** Gādhi travels physically to the kingdom of Kīra and finds that King Kātana’s 60-year reign was a recorded historical fact in the royal archives!

## 7. Rigorous Tripartite Demarcation & Category-Error Refutation

### 7.1 Fallacy 1: Equating *Cidākāśa* Multiverse with Hugh Everett's Many-Worlds Interpretation (MWI)
- **Concordist Claim:** Queen Līlā visiting King Vidūratha proves that the *Yoga-Vāsiṣṭha* discovered Hugh Everett’s 1957 Many-Worlds Interpretation of quantum mechanics.
- **Indological Refutation (Walter Slaje 1994, Jürgen Hanneder 2006):**  
  Everett’s Many-Worlds Interpretation is a strictly deterministic, mathematical interpretation of the Schrödinger equation:
  $$i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} |\Psi(t)\rangle = \hat{H} |\Psi(t)\rangle$$
  In Everettian quantum mechanics, branching occurs when a microscopic quantum system in superposition entangles with an macroscopic apparatus through environmental decoherence. It is **mind-independent**, **unitary**, and **non-subjective**.
  In the *Yoga-Vāsiṣṭha*, there are no quantum wavefunctions, no linear vector spaces, no Planck’s constants ($\hbar$), and no decoherence times ($\tau_D$). Universes are explicitly declared to be **unreal illusions (*mithyā / bhrānti*)** generated by mental ignorance (*avidyā*) and subconscious habits (*vāsanās*). Treating a soteriological allegory designed to dissolve ego-attachment as an anticipation of quantum decoherence is an **untenable category error**.

### 7.2 Fallacy 2: Equating Narrative Time Dilation with Einsteinian Special Relativity
- **Concordist Claim:** The fact that 3 days in Universe 1 equals 70 years in Universe 2 proves Sage Vāsiṣṭha derived the Lorentz factor:
  $$\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}}$$
- **Indological Refutation (B.L. Atreya 1936, Christopher Chapple 1984):**  
  In Special Relativity, time dilation is a symmetric kinematic consequence of the invariance of the speed of light ($c = 299,792,458\text{ m/s}$) across inertial reference frames moving at relative velocity $v$.  
  In the *Yoga-Vāsiṣṭha*, time divergence is **purely psychological and phenomenological**:
