> **यावान्वा अयमाकाशस्तावानेषोऽन्तर्हृदय आकाश उभे अस्मिन्द्यावापृथिवी अन्तरेव समाहिते उभावग्निश्च वायुश्च सूर्याचन्द्रमसावुभौ विद्युन्नक्षत्राणि यच्चास्येहास्ति यच्च नास्ति सर्वं तदस्मिन्समाहितमिति ॥ ३ ॥**
>
> *"Now, here in this city of Brahman (the human body) is a small lotus residence, and within it is a small inner space (*daharo 'ntar-ākāśaḥ*). What is within that must be sought out; that is what one should desire to understand."* (8.1.1)  
> *"If they should ask him: 'Within this city of Brahman, in the small lotus residence, in the small inner space—what exists there that one must seek out?' He should reply:"* (8.1.2)

Within it both heaven and earth are contained, both fire and wind, both the sun and the moon, both lightning and the stars. Whatever exists here, and whatever does not exist, all that is contained within it.**"* (8.1.3)

- **Epistemic Status:** Primary Text (Canonical Vedic Upaniṣad).
