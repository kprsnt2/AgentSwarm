### 5.1 Buddhist Abhidharma Hierarchy (*Vasubandhu*, c. 400–450 CE)

Vasubandhu’s *Abhidharmakośabhāṣya* (Chapter 3, verses 138–140) provides the most mathematically systematic multi-world hierarchy in classical Indian thought:

1. **Single World System (*Cakravāḍa*):** A flat disc centered on Mount Sumeru, bounded by a circular iron mountain rim (*Cakravāḍa-parvata*), diameter $\approx 1,203,450\text{ yojanas}$ ($\sim 1.63\text{ light-years}$).
2. **Small Chiliocosm (*Sāhasra-cūḍika-lokadhātu*):** A collection of $1,000$ world systems ($10^3$), governed by a First Dhyāna Mahābrahmā; radius scales by $\sqrt[3]{1000} = 10\times$ ($\sim 16.3\text{ light-years}$).
3. **Medium Dichiliocosm (*Dvisāhasra-madhyama-lokadhātu*):** A collection of $1,000$ small chiliocosms = $1,000,000$ worlds ($10^6$), illuminated by Second Dhyāna Ābhāsvara Devas; radius scales by $100\times$ ($\sim 163\text{ light-years}$).
4. **Great Trichiliocosm (*Trisāhasra-mahāsāhasra-lokadhātu*):** A collection of $1,000$ medium dichiliocosms = $1,000,000,000$ worlds ($10^9$), encompassing the entire field of transformation (*kṣetra*) of a single Samyaksambuddha; radius scales by $1000\times$ ($\sim 1,630\text{ light-years}$).

In the later Mahāyāna *Avataṃsaka Sūtra* (*Gaṇḍavyūha*), this expands into the infinite **Lotus Treasury World (*Padmagarbha-lokadhātu*)** and the metaphor of **Indra’s Net (*Indrajāla*)**, where infinite jewels reflect all other jewels, creating a holographic, mutually interpenetrating multiverse.

### 5.2 Jaina Cosmology: The Bounded Cosmos in Infinite Void

In Umāsvāti’s *Tattvārtha Sūtra* and Yativṛṣabha’s *Tiloyapaṇṇatti*, cosmology is bifurcated into two absolute domains:
1. **The Loka (Cosmos):** A finite, spindle-shaped structure shaped like a person standing with legs apart and hands on hips.
   - Total Height: $14\text{ Raju}$.
   - Total Volume: Exactly $343\text{ Raju}^3$.
   - Contents: Contains all matter (*pudgala*), conscious souls (*jīva*), motion-medium (*dharma*), rest-medium (*adharma*), and time (*kāla*).
2. **The Aloka-ākāśa (Non-Cosmos):** The unbounded, infinite space surrounding the Loka.
   - Contains strictly **zero matter** and **zero souls** ($\text{Matter} = 0$, $\text{Jīvas} = 0$).
   - Pure, unconditioned, empty space (*śuddha-ākāśa*).
   - In Jainism, there are **no multiple physical Lokas**; the universe is strictly singular and uncreated, but temporally infinite through cyclic oscillations (*utsarpiṇī* ascending and *avasarpiṇī* descending half-cycles).

### 5.3 Indological Genealogy of the Hindu Multiverse

Scholarly consensus (Willibald Kirfel 1920, Richard Gombrich 1975, Randy Kloetzli 1983, Johannes Bronkhorst 2007) establishes the following evolutionary trajectory:
1. **Early Vedic Period (c. 1500–800 BCE):** Only a single, tripartite world (*Tribhuvana*: Earth, Atmospheric Mid-space, Celestial Sky) is known (*Rigveda* 10.90, 10.129). No multiverses exist.
2. **Śramaṇic Challenge (c. 500–200 BCE):** Early Buddhism and Jainism reject a Vedic creator God and posit enormous temporal eons (*Kalpas*, *Sāgaropamas*) and multi-world assemblies ($10^9$ worlds in Buddhism) to reflect the boundless karmic transmigrations of sentient beings.
3. **Purāṇic Synthesis & Hegemonic Expansion (c. 300–900 CE):** Encountering the colossal scale of Buddhist-Jain multiverses, Brahmanical redactors dramatically expanded the Hindu *Brahmāṇḍa*. Rather than conceding cosmological superiority to Buddhism, the Purāṇic authors asserted that **innumerable billions of such universes (*aneka-koṭi-brahmāṇḍa*)** originate as microscopic bubbles from the pores of Mahā-Viṣṇu or the smile of Śiva, thereby subordinating the entire Buddhist-Jain cosmic expanse inside the body of the Hindu Supreme Deity!

## 7. Indological Safeguards and Protocol Verification

### 7.1 Safeguard 1: Scripture Is Not Laboratory Data
- We reject all claims that the *Bhāgavata Purāṇa* or *Yoga Vāsiṣṭha* contain modern quantum field theory or Einsteinian general relativity.
- When computing $\gamma = 1.278 \times 10^{12}$ or $v \approx (1 - 3.06 \times 10^{-25})c$, we are evaluating the **physical consequences that would result if the literary parameters were treated as physical claims**, conclusively proving that the narrative is a theological-literary mythos, not an engineering or physical flight report.

## 8. Established Ground Truths, Known Unknowns, and Falsification Criteria

### 8.1 Established Ground Truths
1. **Observer-Dependent Temporal Hierarchy:** Classical Hindu thought possessed an articulated mathematical ladder of observer-dependent time scaling across 6 distinct cosmological planes, spanning factors from $1.0$ (terrestrial) to $3.1104 \times 10^{14}$ (Mahā-Kalpa).
2. **Kakudmi Relativity Parameter:** The Kakudmi-Revatī voyage in *Bhāgavata* 9.3 corresponds to a theoretical time-dilation factor $\gamma = 1.278 \times 10^{12}$, which modern physics demonstrates is physically unlivable due to CMB blueshift vaporization ($6.97 \times 10^{12}\text{ K}$, $1.8\text{ GeV}$ photons).
3. **Co-Spatial Fractal Multiverse:** The *Yoga Vāsiṣṭha* formulated the doctrine that parallel universes can occupy identical spatial coordinates without mutual collision, separated solely by consciousness tuning (*Cidākāśa*).
4. **Pan-Indic Structural Divergence:** The three major Indian traditions developed three radically distinct cosmological topologies: Hindu (theistic bubble archipelago), Buddhist (non-theistic tiered trichiliocosms), and Jaina (bounded singular Loka in infinite Aloka).
5. **Indological Evolutionary Arc:** The Puranic *aneka-koṭi-brahmāṇḍa* doctrine was redacted between 300 and 1000 CE partly to encompass and supersede the staggering scales of Buddhist and Jain cosmologies.

### 8.2 Known Unknowns
1. **Exact Transmission Mechanism between Buddhist Abhidharma and Early Purāṇas:** While structural parallels between Buddhist $10^9$-world trichiliocosms and Puranic *aneka-koṭi-brahmāṇḍa* are clear, the precise literary vectors of borrowing between Buddhist monasteries (like Nālandā) and Brahmanical purāṇic redactors between 200 and 600 CE remain undocumented.
2. **Transmission of Yoga Vāsiṣṭha Idealism to Kashmir Śaivism:** The degree of mutual textual borrowing regarding parallel consciousness realms (*spanda-tattva*) between the *Yoga Vāsiṣṭha* and the Trika Śaiva philosophers (Abhinavagupta, Kṣemarāja) remains partially open.

### 8.3 What Evidence Would Falsify or Revise this Assessment
1. **Discovery of Pre-Buddhist Vedic Texts Positing Aneka-Brahmāṇḍa:** If an undiscovered Vedic Saṁhitā or early Upaniṣad (prior to 600 BCE) is discovered explicitly describing multiple *Brahmāṇḍas*, our model of Buddhist-induced competitive expansion would be falsified.
2. **Discovery of Empirical Observational Instruments in Jyotiṣa:** If astronomical texts by Āryabhaṭa, Brahmagupta, or Bhāskara are found describing empirical telescopes or observations of parallel universes, the firewall separating mathematical astronomy from purāṇic mythos would be revised.
