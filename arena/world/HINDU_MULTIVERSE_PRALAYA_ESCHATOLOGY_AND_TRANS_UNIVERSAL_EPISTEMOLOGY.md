In classical Puranic and philosophical literature, universal dissolution is not a singular, uniform collapse. Primary texts (*Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 12.4.1–38, *Viṣṇu Purāṇa* 6.3–4, *Matsya Purāṇa* 167) formalize an exact four-tiered hierarchy of *pralaya*:

```
   Kāla-pariṇāma       Saṁvartaka Fire & Flood              Reverse Sāṅkhya      Brahma-Jñāna
```

### 2.1 *Nitya Pralaya* (Perpetual / Microscopic Continuous Dissolution)
- **Primary Text:** *Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 12.4.35 (*kṣaṇādīnām... pariṇāmaḥ*); *Viṣṇu Purāṇa* 6.3.39–41.
- **Scope & Timescale:** Operates continuously at sub-atomic, molecular, and biological scales ($\tau \sim 10^{-9}\text{ to }10^2\text{ s}$).
- **Mechanism:** The unrelenting transformative flux of Time (*Kāla-pariṇāma*). Every metabolic birth or material aggregation is simultaneously an unceasing dissolution of prior states.

### 2.2 *Naimittika Pralaya* (Occasional / Kalpa-End Dissolution)
- **Primary Text:** *Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 12.4.2–6; *Viṣṇu Purāṇa* 6.3.1–38; *Kūrma Purāṇa* 2.45.
- **Scope & Recurrence:** Occurs at the conclusion of each daytime period of Brahmā ($1\text{ Kalpa} = 1,000\text{ Mahāyugas} = 4.32 \times 10^9\text{ solar years}$). Across the 100-year lifespan of a Brahmā, this occurs exactly **$36,000\text{ times}$** (with 36,000 alternating dawns).
- **Subsystems Affected:** Confined strictly to the lower three planetary systems (*Trailokya*): **Bhūr-loka**, **Bhuvar-loka**, and **Svar-loka** ($\frac{3}{14} \approx 21.4\%$ of the vertical cosmic architecture).
- **Thermal & Hydrological Kinematics:**
  1. **100-Year Drought (*Anāvṛṣṭi*):** Complete absence of precipitation across the lower planetary spheres.
  2. **The Seven Suns (*Sapta-Raśmayaḥ*):** Solar luminosity intensifies by a factor of $7\times$, evaporating oceans, rivers, and planetary moisture (*Viṣṇu Purāṇa* 6.3.14–18).
  3. **The Saṅkarṣaṇa Subterranean Fire:** Blazing subterranean fire emanates from the mouth of Śeṣa/Saṅkarṣaṇa, burning the subterranean realms (*Pātālas*) and rising through the Earth and sky.
  4. **The Evacuation of Maharloka:** Due to extreme radiant heat flux, perfected beings residing on Mahar-loka cannot endure the radiation and ascend to Jana-loka (*Bhāgavata* 12.4.5).
  5. **The 100-Year Torrential Deluge (*Saṁvartaka* Clouds):** The doom-clouds—named *Puṣkara*, *Āvarta*, *Droṇa*, and *Saṁvarta*—pour torrential rain for 100 consecutive solar years, submerging the three worlds into one vast cosmic sea (*Ekārṇava*), while Brahmā sleeps upon Ananta-Śeṣa.

### 2.3 *Prākṛtika Pralaya* (Elemental / Universal Collapse)
- **Primary Text:** *Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 12.4.7–22; *Viṣṇu Purāṇa* 6.4.1–43.
- **Scope & Timescale:** Occurs upon the expiration of Brahmā's lifespan ($100\text{ divine years} / 2\text{ Parārdhas} = 72,000\text{ Kalpas} = 3.1104 \times 10^{14}\text{ solar years}$).
- **Subsystems Affected:** Total annihilation of all 14 Lokas and the entire 7-sheath outer envelope. The individualized Brahmāṇḍa completely collapses back into unmanifest Prakṛti.

### 2.4 *Ātyantika Pralaya* (Absolute Ontological Emancipation)
- **Primary Text:** *Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 12.4.23–34; *Brahma Sūtras* 4.4; *Yoga Vāsiṣṭha* 6.
- **Scope & Timescale:** Non-temporal and irreversible ($\tau = \infty$). It is achieved when an individual soul (*Jīva*) attains direct intuitive realization of Brahman (*Brahma-sākṣātkāra*), terminating ignorance (*Avidyā*). For that Jīva, the entire illusory display of the multiverse permanently ceases to exist (*na sa punar āvartate*).
