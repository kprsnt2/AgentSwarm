## 5. Stratigraphy of the Bhagavad Gītā

The *Bhagavad Gītā* (Bhīṣma Parva, chapters 23–40 of the Critical Edition) is neither an alien interpolated tract nor a literal stenographic transcript of a 700-verse philosophical discourse delivered on an active battlefield line. Philological analysis reveals three organic developmental strata:

1. **Stratum 1: The Core Saṃjaya Dialogue (c. 500–400 BCE)**
   - *Scope:* Chapters 1 to 2.38.
   - *Grammar & Metre:* High ratio of archaic Triṣṭubh metres, close to Late Vedic Brahmana prose.
   - *Content:* Arjuna’s moral despondency (*viṣāda*) over fratricide; Krishna’s reply rooted in early Sāṃkhya dualism (eternality of the *ātman*, mortality of the physical body) and the Kshatriya *svadharma* of righteous warfare.
   - *Persona of Krishna:* Human mentor, royal prince, and chariot-driver (*Pārthasārathi*).

2. **Stratum 2: Classical Upaniṣadic Synthesis (c. 400–200 BCE)**
   - *Scope:* Chapters 2.39 to 9.
   - *Grammar & Metre:* Classical epic Anuṣṭubh ślokas with standardized Sandhi rules.
   - *Content:* Integration of *Karma-yoga* (unattached action), *Jñāna-yoga*, and Vedantic monism directly quoting or paraphrasing the Kaṭha and Īśa Upaniṣads.
   - *Persona of Krishna:* Spiritual Guru and divine teacher of selfless sacrifice (*Niṣkāma Karma*).

3. **Stratum 3: Vaiṣṇava Theophany & Bhakti Synthesis (c. 200 BCE – 100 CE)**
   - *Scope:* Chapters 10–12 (especially Chapter 11 *Viśvarūpa Darśana*) and 13–18.
   - *Grammar & Metre:* Highly ornate Triṣṭubh hymns of awe and submission; complex theological compounds.
   - *Content:* Direct revelation of Krishna as the cosmic ground of being (*Brahman*), *Puruṣottama*, and the devouring form of Time (*Kāla*). Total surrender (*Śaraṇāgati*, BG 18.66).
   - *Persona of Krishna:* *Svayam Bhagavān*, identical with Viṣṇu-Nārāyaṇa.

This internal stratigraphy perfectly mirrors the broader macro-evolution of Indian religion from Vedic sacrificial ritualism to Upaniṣadic interior contemplation to classic Bhakti devotional theism.

## 6. What Is Established, What Remains Unknown, and What Evidence Would Change Our Mind

### 6.1 What Is Established Beyond Reasonable Scientific Doubt
1. **Historical Human Core for Krishna:** A real chieftain, statesman, and teacher named Krishna Vāsudeva of the Vṛṣṇi/Yādava lineage lived in northern India (Mathura/Suraseni region) c. 1000–850 BCE.
2. **Historical Core for the Kurukṣetra Conflict:** A real dynastic fratricidal civil war occurred among the Bharata/Kuru lineages in the Upper Gangetic Doab during the Early Iron Age / PGW cultural period (c. 1000–850 BCE), involving $\sim 15,000\text{--}30,000$ warriors with bloomery iron weapons and light horse chariots.
3. **Accretive Textual Stratigraphy:** The epic grew across 1,200 years from an original bardic lay (*Jaya*, $\sim 8,800$ verses) to an aristocratic epic (*Bhārata*, $\sim 24,000$ verses) to an encyclopedic Dharma-śāstra (*Mahābhārata*, $\sim 100,000$ verses) finalized in the Gupta era.
4. **3102 BCE as Astronomical Retro-Calculation:** The traditional Kali Yuga date of 3102 BCE is an astronomical back-calculation formulated by Aryabhaṭa in 499 CE based on mean planetary conjunctions, not a surviving contemporary calendar date.
5. **Apotheosis Trajectory:** Epigraphic monuments (Agathocles coins, Heliodorus pillar, Mora Well) trace the physical, visible transformation of Krishna from a venerated clan hero to the Supreme Lord over eight centuries.

### 6.2 What Remains Unknown
1. **The Exact Year of the Kurukṣetra Conflict:** While firmly bounded between 1000 and 850 BCE by archaeological strata, ceramic evolution, and flood siltation, it cannot currently be pinned down to a specific calendar year or day.
2. **The Exact Boundaries of Krishna's Military Role:** Did Krishna act solely as a diplomat and strategist (as depicted in the epic), or did he personally command forces in earlier lost versions of the *Jaya*?
3. **The Precise Underwater Architecture at Dwārkā:** While stone anchors and Late Harappan pottery exist off Bet Dwārkā, whether structural stone remnants underwater represent ancient wharves or submerged natural littoral formations remains partially contested among marine archaeologists.

### 6.3 What Specific Evidence Would Change Our Mind
1. **To overturn the Historical Core in favor of Pure Myth:**
   - Radiocarbon proof that PGW settlement sites at Kurukshetra, Hastinapur, and Mathura were sterile wilderness until the 4th century BCE.
   - Discovery that the *Chāndogya Upaniṣad*, Pāṇini, and Megasthenes fragments mentioning Vāsudeva/Krishna are post-Mauryan fabrications.
2. **To overturn the Early Iron Age Chronology in favor of Traditional 3102 BCE Literalism:**
   - Discovery of an in situ literate Sanskrit inscription written in an early Indic script dated securely via radiocarbon to $\sim 3100$ BCE.
   - Excavation of authentic spoked-wheel war chariots and domesticated horse remains (*Equus caballus*) in third-millennium BCE Harappan levels in the Gangetic plain.
   - Evidence of bloomery iron smelting technology mass-producing shafted weapons prior to 3000 BCE in northern India.
