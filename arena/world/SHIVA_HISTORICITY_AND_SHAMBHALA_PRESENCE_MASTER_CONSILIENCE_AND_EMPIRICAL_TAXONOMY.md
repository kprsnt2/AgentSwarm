### 2.2 Unbroken 3,500-Year Philological Stratigraphy

The historical emergence of Shiva is documented across five rigorous textual strata:

1. **Vedic Stratum (c. 1500–800 BCE) `[P]`:**
   * *Rigveda 10.92.9:* The term *śiva* (PIE $\sqrt{*\text{ḱoi-wo-}}$, "auspicious, propitious, dear") first appears as an adjectival epithet applied to the fierce storm god Rudra (*rudrāya śivāya*).
   * *Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā 16 (Śatarudrīya) / Taittirīya Saṃhitā 4.5:* Rudra-Shiva is addressed as the universal indweller: dweller of the mountains (*giriśanta*), lord of artisans, carpenters, thieves, forests, and wild beasts. Formulaic litany: *namaḥ śivāya ca śivatarāya ca* ("salutations to the auspicious one, and to the more auspicious one").
   * *Atharvaveda 11.2:* Systematizes the Eight Forms (*Aṣṭamūrti*): Bhava, Śarva, Paśupati, Ugra, Mahādeva, Rudra, Īśāna, and Aśani.

2. **Upanishadic Stratum (c. 600–300 BCE) `[P]`:**
   * *Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad (3.11, 4.10, 6.7):* Shiva transitions from a Vedic deity into the Supreme Transcendent Brahman and Lord of Māyā (*māyāṁ tu prakṛtiṁ vidyān māyinaṁ tu maheśvaram*). Synthesizes pre-classical Sāṃkhya categories with theistic personal monism.

3. **Grammatical & Classical Epigraphic Stratum (c. 4th c. BCE – 2nd c. CE) `[P]` `[S]`:**
   * *Pāṇini's Aṣṭādhyāyī (5.2.76, 4.1.49):* Explicitly codifies devotees of Shiva (*Śiva-bhāgavatas*) and ascetics bearing iron spears/tridents (*ayaḥśūlika*).
   * *Patañjali's Mahābhāṣya (c. 150 BCE):* Confirms historical sectarian ascetics carrying iron lances (*śūla*) and animal hides (*ajina*).
   * *Gudimallam Lingam (Andhra Pradesh, c. 2nd–1st c. BCE):* Earliest extant stone anthropomorphic lingam depicting Shiva standing atop the dwarf yakṣa Apasmāra, holding a water vessel, axe, and antelope.

4. **Kushan & Pan-Asian Imperial Numismatics (c. 1st–4th c. CE) `[P]` `[S]`:**
   * *Kushan Coinage (Vima Kadphises, Kanishka, Huvishka):* Gold and copper imperial issues bearing the deity *Oesho* (Bactrian for *Vṛṣa* or *Īśa* / *Maheśvara*), depicted ithyphallic with trident (*triśūla*), water vessel, and the humped bull Nandi. Royal legend: *maharajasa rajadirajasa sarvaloga isvarasa mahisvarasa vima kathphishasa tratara* ("Great king of kings, Lord of all the world, Maheśvara...").

5. **Philosophical Trika & Saiddhāntika Stratum (c. 800–1100 CE) `[P]`:**
   * *Kashmir Shaivism (Vasugupta, Utpaladeva, Abhinavagupta):* Formulates Shiva as *Prakāśa* (unconditioned self-luminous consciousness) inseparable from *Vimarśa* (spontaneous creative reflexivity). Reality is defined as *Caitanya* (Consciousness itself).
