### 3.1 Theorem 1: Zero Fisher Information ($I(\theta_{\text{meta}}) \equiv 0.0$)

Let an empirical observation vector registered by physical instruments be $Y \in \mathcal{Y}_{\text{obs}}$, governed by a conditional probability density function $f(y; \theta_{\text{phys}}, \theta_{\text{meta}})$, where:

* $\theta_{\text{meta}} \in \{0, 1\}$ represents the metaphysical parameter (e.g., whether the unconditioned Ground of Consciousness underlies physical phenomena, or whether a subtle Pure Land coexists uncoupled from the electromagnetic spectrum).

Because metaphysical doctrines explicitly define divine action and subtle planes as operating *through* natural physical regularities (theological concealment / non-electromagnetic coupling), the likelihood of observing any physical dataset $Y$ is strictly invariant under changes in $\theta_{\text{meta}}$:

$$f(y; \theta_{\text{phys}}, \theta_{\text{meta}}) \equiv f(y; \theta_{\text{phys}}) \quad \forall y \in \mathcal{Y}_{\text{obs}}$$

The score function $S(\theta_{\text{meta}})$ is the gradient of the log-likelihood:

$$S(\theta_{\text{meta}}) = \frac{\partial \ln f(y; \theta_{\text{phys}}, \theta_{\text{meta}})}{\partial \theta_{\text{meta}}} = \frac{\partial \ln f(y; \theta_{\text{phys}})}{\partial \theta_{\text{meta}}} \equiv 0$$

The Fisher Information $I(\theta_{\text{meta}})$ of empirical physical data with respect to the metaphysical parameter is therefore identically zero:

$$I(\theta_{\text{meta}}) = \mathbb{E}\left[ \left( S(\theta_{\text{meta}}) \right)^2 \right] = \mathbb{E}[0^2] \equiv 0.0$$

$$\text{Var}(\hat{\theta}_{\text{meta}}) \ge \frac{1}{I(\theta_{\text{meta}})} = \frac{1}{0.0} = \infty$$

Under Bayesian inference, the Shannon information gain $\Delta I$ in bits imparted by an empirical dataset $E$ between the metaphysical hypothesis $H_{\text{meta}}$ and physical naturalism $H_{\text{nat}}$ is given by the logarithm of the likelihood ratio:

$$\Lambda = \frac{\mathcal{P}(E \mid H_{\text{meta}})}{\mathcal{P}(E \mid H_{\text{nat}})} = \frac{\mathcal{P}(E)}{\mathcal{P}(E)} = 1.0$$

$$\Delta I = \log_2(\Lambda) = \log_2(1.0) \equiv 0.0 \text{ bits}$$

$$D_{\text{KL}}(\mathcal{P}(E \mid H_{\text{meta}}) \parallel \mathcal{P}(E \mid H_{\text{nat}})) = \int_{\mathcal{Y}} f(y \mid H_{\text{meta}}) \ln \frac{f(y \mid H_{\text{meta}})}{f(y \mid H_{\text{nat}})} \, dy = \int_{\mathcal{Y}} f(y) \ln(1.0) \, dy \equiv 0.0$$
