  • Solar Eclipse: The Moon occults the solar disk.
  • Rahu and Ketu: Re-conceptualized as the two geometric ascending and descending orbital nodes where the lunar orbital plane intersects the ecliptic (lunar orbital inclination $i \approx 4^\circ 30'$ in Aryabhatiya, modern $5^\circ 9'$).

Let $D_{\odot} = 1,392,700\text{ km}$, $D_{\oplus} = 12,742\text{ km}$, $R_{\odot\oplus} = 149,597,870\text{ km}$, and $d_{\text{moon}} = 384,400\text{ km}$. The length of the Earth's conical umbra $L$ is: $$L = \frac{R_{\odot\oplus} \cdot r_{\oplus}}{r_{\odot} - r_{\oplus}} = \frac{149,597,870 \times 6,371}{696,350 - 6,371} \approx 1,381,329\text{ km}$$ The diameter of the umbra at the Moon's distance is: $$D_{\text{umbra}} = D_{\oplus} \left(1 - \frac{d_{\text{moon}}}{L}\right) = 12,742 \left(1 - \frac{384,400}{1,381,329}\right) \approx 9,196.1\text{ km}$$ Because the Moon's diameter is $D_{\text{moon}} = 3,474\text{ km}$, the shadow coverage ratio is: $$\text{Coverage Ratio} = \frac{D_{\text{umbra}}}{D_{\text{moon}}} = \frac{9,196.1}{3,474} \approx 2.647 > 1.0$$ This demonstrates that total lunar eclipses are physically inevitable whenever the Moon passes near the node during opposition, demythologizing demon consumption into celestial mechanics.

4.3 Dignaga and Dharmakirti’s Buddhist Dismantling of Theism

Buddhist logicians (Dignaga, Dharmakirti in Pramanavarttika) systematically dismantled the Nyaya cosmological argument for a creator God:

  • The Nyaya school argued: "The universe, mountains, and rivers are effects; like pots, they must have an intelligent maker (Ishvara)."
  • Dharmakirti demonstrated this argument commits the fallacy of **unestablished analogy (Asiddha)**: A pot is produced by a visible, embodied human artisan using physical tools in time. Transferring this observation to infer an unembodied, omniscient, eternal creator of mountains and oceans is an illicit inductive leap. Nature exhibits self-organizing dependent co-arising (Pratityasamutpada), requiring no supreme personal deity.

4.4 The Purva Mimamsa Paradox: Orthodox Atheism

Purva Mimamsa (Jaimini, Kumarila Bhatta, Prabhakara) established a critical insight for the epistemic demarcation of religion:

  • They defended the absolute eternal authority of the Vedas (Apaurusheyatva).
  • Yet they **explicitly rejected the existence of a creator God (Ishvara)**, rejected the cosmic creation and destruction (Srishti-Pralaya), and affirmed that the universe has always existed (na kadacid anidrsham jagat - "the world was never not like this").
  • Devas (Indra, Agni, Varuna) were reduced to linguistic mantras and syllables (shabda-matra) necessary for the performance of sacrificial duties (vidhis), possessing no physical or subtle ontological existence.
  • Epistemic Finding: Within orthodox (Astika) Hindu thought itself, reverence for sacred texts was formally severed from belief in an omnipotent creator God. Theism and scriptural tradition are structurally independent.

5. The Structural Mechanics of Why Metaphysical Claims Resist Empirical Testing

Why do Class B claims resist empirical adjudication? This resistance is not a contingent limitation of current instrumentation; it is a structural property of the propositions themselves.

5.1 Category Mismatch of Physical Instrumentation

Physical instruments register detection events via exchange of mass-energy or momentum: $$\Delta p \cdot \Delta x \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}$$ Interactions must be mediated by the four fundamental forces: electromagnetic, strong nuclear, weak nuclear, or gravitational. In contrast, classical Dharmic metaphysics defines Brahman and Atman as:

  • Atindriya: Beyond sensory organs and physical coupling.
  • Nirguna: Possessing zero physical attributes, charges, or masses.
  • Avyakta: Unmanifest subjective observer rather than an observed object.

An entity defined as lacking physical coupling cannot exchange quanta with a detector. Physical non-detection cannot serve as evidence of absence.

5.2 The Bayesian Likelihood Invariance Theorem

Let $D \in \mathcal{D}$ represent any possible empirical dataset regarding the physical universe (e.g., fundamental constants, cosmic microwave background, DNA structure, fossil records, thermodynamic entropy, or distribution of human suffering).

Let $H_0$ be the physicalist-naturalist hypothesis (spacetime and mass-energy fields are ontological primitives). Let $H_1$ be the metaphysical theism/Brahman hypothesis (ultimate consciousness manifests the universe).

Because the believer model interprets every discovery of physical order, constant fine-tuning, or mathematical law as the manifestation of divine will (Ishvara / Leela / Rta / Vyavaharika Satya), both models assign identical likelihoods to the empirical observations: $$P(D \mid H_1) = P(D \mid H_0)$$

The Likelihood Ratio $\Lambda(D)$ is: $$\Lambda(D) \equiv \frac{P(D \mid H_1)}{P(D \mid H_0)} = 1.0000$$ The Log-Bayes Factor is: $$\ln(\text{BF}) = \ln(1.0) = 0$$ The Information Gain (Kullback-Leibler divergence) is: $$D_{\text{KL}}(P(D \mid H_1) \parallel P(D \mid H_0)) = \int P(D \mid H_1) \ln\left(\frac{P(D \mid H_1)}{P(D \mid H_0)}\right) dD = 0\text{ bits}$$

Applying Bayes' Theorem: $$\frac{P(H_1 \mid D)}{P(H_0 \mid D)} = \frac{P(H_1)}{P(H_0)} \times \Lambda(D) = \frac{P(H_1)}{P(H_0)}$$

Mathematical Conclusion: The posterior odds ratio is identically equal to the prior odds ratio. No empirical observation in physical spacetime can alter the relative probability of the two hypotheses.

5.3 Post-Hoc Causal Insulation and Overfitting in Karmic Accounting

The trans-migratory moral causality model (Karma and Samsara) asserts that unearned human suffering or fortune is caused by unseen moral debts (Adrishta) generated in previous incarnations.

Mathematically, let an individual's observed life outcome $Y_i$ (e.g., health, wealth, tragedy) be modeled as: $$Y_i = \mathbf{X}_i \boldsymbol{\beta} + K_i + \epsilon_i$$ where $\mathbf{X}_i$ is a vector of $p$ observable biological, genetic, and socioeconomic variables, $\boldsymbol{\beta}$ are causal weights, $\epsilon_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ is stochastic variation, and $K_i$ is the karmic maturation (Prarabdha Karma) from unobserved past lives.

  • Under naturalism: $K_i \equiv 0$. The model has $p$ parameters, leaving $N - p > 0$ residual degrees of freedom. It makes falsifiable predictions about outcome distributions and variance.
  • Under the karmic model: Because past lives are unobservable in the laboratory, $K_i$ is a free latent variable assigned post-hoc for each individual:

$$\hat{K}_i \equiv Y_i - \mathbf{X}_i \boldsymbol{\beta}$$ The number of estimated parameters is $p + N \ge N$. The residual degrees of freedom are: $$\text{df} = N - (p + N) = -p \le 0$$

Because an unconstrained latent parameter exists for every observation, the karmic hypothesis can absorb any arbitrary distribution of suffering or fortune without risk of refutation. It possesses zero predictive constraint over the observation space, rendering it unfalsifiable.

5.4 Epistemic Circularity of Scriptural Self-Validation (Svatah-Pramanya)

Purva Mimamsa and Vedanta establish scriptural validity via Svatah-pramanya (intrinsic validity):

  • An epistemic instrument validates itself within its own domain.
  • Sensory perception (Pratyaksha) validates physical objects; scriptural testimony (Sabda) validates suprasensory truths.
  • To judge scripture by empirical science is rejected as a category error; to judge empirical science by scripture is equally rejected.

This creates a self-enclosed epistemic circle: believers operate within the axiomatic closure of Sabda, while non-believers reject the foundational validity of the instrument.
