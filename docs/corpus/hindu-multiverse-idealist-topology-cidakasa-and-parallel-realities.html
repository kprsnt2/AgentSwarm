7. Rigorous Tripartite Demarcation & Category-Error Refutation

7.1 Fallacy 1: Equating Cidākāśa Multiverse with Hugh Everett's Many-Worlds Interpretation (MWI)

  • Concordist Claim: Queen Līlā visiting King Vidūratha proves that the Yoga-Vāsiṣṭha discovered Hugh Everett’s 1957 Many-Worlds Interpretation of quantum mechanics.
  • Indological Refutation (Walter Slaje 1994, Jürgen Hanneder 2006):

Everett’s Many-Worlds Interpretation is a strictly deterministic, mathematical interpretation of the Schrödinger equation: $$i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} |\Psi(t)\rangle = \hat{H} |\Psi(t)\rangle$$ In Everettian quantum mechanics, branching occurs when a microscopic quantum system in superposition entangles with an macroscopic apparatus through environmental decoherence. It is mind-independent, unitary, and non-subjective. In the Yoga-Vāsiṣṭha, there are no quantum wavefunctions, no linear vector spaces, no Planck’s constants ($\hbar$), and no decoherence times ($\tau_D$). Universes are explicitly declared to be **unreal illusions (mithyā / bhrānti)** generated by mental ignorance (avidyā) and subconscious habits (vāsanās). Treating a soteriological allegory designed to dissolve ego-attachment as an anticipation of quantum decoherence is an untenable category error.

7.2 Fallacy 2: Equating Narrative Time Dilation with Einsteinian Special Relativity

  • Concordist Claim: The fact that 3 days in Universe 1 equals 70 years in Universe 2 proves Sage Vāsiṣṭha derived the Lorentz factor:

$$\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}}$$

  • Indological Refutation (B.L. Atreya 1936, Christopher Chapple 1984):

In Special Relativity, time dilation is a symmetric kinematic consequence of the invariance of the speed of light ($c = 299,792,458\text{ m/s}$) across inertial reference frames moving at relative velocity $v$. In the Yoga-Vāsiṣṭha, time divergence is purely psychological and phenomenological:

manaḥ-kalpitam evaitat kāla-saṅkalpanam mahat | kṣaṇo yugāyate kasmin yugaṁ kṣaṇāyate kvacit || "This vast conception of time is merely imagined by the mind; to one person a single moment seems like an eon, while to another an eon seems like a moment." — Yoga-Vāsiṣṭha 3.20.25

This is the familiar psychological phenomenon of dream-time (where a 5-minute REM dream feels like hours), not an invariant tensor metric in pseudo-Riemannian spacetime ($g_{\mu\nu}$).

7.3 Fallacy 3: Worlds within Atoms (Paramāṇv-Antar-Gata Jagat) as Quantum String Vacua

  • Concordist Claim: The verse paramāṇu-madhye 'pi jaganti santi ("even inside an atom there are worlds") proves knowledge of subatomic particles and string theory extra dimensions.
  • Indological Refutation:

The paramāṇu in Yoga-Vāsiṣṭha is not a physical chemical atom or a quark; it is a rhetorical device for the **infinitesimal point of consciousness (cid-aṇu)**. The text repeatedly insists that these inner worlds have no physical mass, generate no gravity, and cannot be detected by physical instruments because they exist solely as mental dreams within consciousness space (Cidākāśa).
