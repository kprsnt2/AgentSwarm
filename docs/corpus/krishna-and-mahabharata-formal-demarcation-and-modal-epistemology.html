4. Information-Theoretic Horizon: Vanishing Fisher Information & Infinite Estimator Variance

Why does the assertion "Lord Krishna is real (as God)" resist empirical testing with mathematical certainty? We formalize this resistance using classical estimation theory and Fisher Information.

Let $\theta \in \Theta$ represent the ontological parameter:

  • $\theta = 0$: Naturalistic Historical Baseline (mortal human, poetic accretion, cultural myth).
  • $\theta = 1$: Transcendent Divine Reality (unconditioned Godhead incarnate in history).

Let $X = \{x_1, x_2, \dots, x_N\}$ denote the set of all possible physical, geological, taphonomic, archaeological, and epigraphic observables collected across all epic sites.

Under orthodox theological doctrine (Bhagavad Gītā 4.6, 7.25, 9.11), the divine descent operates under the veil of Yoga-māyā:

avajānanti māṁ mūḍhā mānuṣīṁ tanum āśritam ("Fools deride Me when I descend in human form, unaware of My transcendent nature.")

The theology explicitly posits that the physical body, biological life, and material traces of the Avatāra conform to the ordinary empirical laws of the spatiotemporal world. Therefore, the conditional probability density of observing material data $X$ given $\theta$ is identical under both hypotheses: $$p(X \mid \theta = 1) \equiv p(X \mid \theta = 0) \quad \forall X$$

1. Log-Likelihood Ratio

$$\Lambda(X) = \ln \left( \frac{p(X \mid \theta = 1)}{p(X \mid \theta = 0)} \right) = \ln(1) = 0$$

2. Score Function

The score function $\mathcal{S}(\theta)$ is the gradient of the log-likelihood: $$\mathcal{S}(\theta) = \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} \ln p(X \mid \theta) = 0 \quad \forall X$$

3. Fisher Information

The Fisher Information $I(\theta)$, measuring the amount of information that observable $X$ carries about unknown parameter $\theta$, is: $$I(\theta) = \mathbb{E} \left[ \left( \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} \ln p(X \mid \theta) \right)^2 \right] = \mathbb{E}[0^2] = 0$$

4. The Cramér-Rao Lower Bound

By the Cramér-Rao Inequality, the variance of any unbiased empirical estimator $\hat{\theta}(X)$ attempting to measure the reality of divine avatarhood is bounded from below: $$\text{Var}(\hat{\theta}) \ge \frac{1}{N \cdot I(\theta)} = \frac{1}{N \cdot 0} \to \infty$$

5. Shannon Mutual Information

The mutual information $I(X; \Theta)$ between physical measurements $X$ and metaphysical reality $\Theta$ is: $$I(X; \Theta) = H(X) - H(X \mid \Theta) = 0\text{ bits}$$

Mathematical Conclusion: No increase in the volume of empirical data ($N \to \infty$), no precision of mass spectrometry, and no resolution of satellite remote sensing can reduce the variance of an empirical estimator of divine ontology below infinity. The claim is fundamentally information-theoretically silent to empirical instruments.
