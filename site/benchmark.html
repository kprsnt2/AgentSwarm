Experimental Methodology

1 · Standardized Research Task

Every agent was prompted with: “Is commercial fusion power achievable by 2040? Rank confinement schemes, tritium breeding limits, and private sector timelines.”

2 · Equal Turn Budget

Both substrates were given exactly 1 turn with an identical 30-minute maximum execution timeout to prevent runaway resource exhaustion.

3 · Forensic Audit Verification

Every run was monitored by the dual-oracle verifying that zero phantom artifacts were claimed and that written files adhered to physical conservation laws.
