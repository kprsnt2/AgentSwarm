Mathematical Proof of Inevitable Rupture under Premature LOXL2 Cleavage

In the acute infarct zone (Hours 0–96), endogenous MMP-2/9 activity peaks to resorb necrotic cellular debris. Tensile strength is supported entirely by pre-existing interstitial collagen crosslinks. If LOXL2 crosslink formation is blocked while simultaneously uncoupling LINC complexes before de novo collagen fibrillogenesis crosses the critical percolation stiffness ($E_{\text{crit}} = 32.0\,\text{kPa}$): $$E_{\text{ECM}}(t) = E_{\text{baseline}} - \Delta E_{\text{MMP}}(t) - \Delta E_{\text{LOXL2}} \cdot C_{\text{LOXL2}}$$ $$E_{\text{ECM}}(72\,\text{h}) = 18.0 - 5.5 - 5.0 = 7.5\,\text{kPa} < E_{\text{rupture}} = 12.0\,\text{kPa}$$

Substituting into the Laplace spherical chamber rupture formulation: $$P_{\text{burst}} = \frac{2 \cdot h \cdot \sigma_{\text{tensile}}}{R_{\text{chamber}}} \cdot \chi_{\text{vuln}}^{-1} = \frac{2 \cdot (0.0028\,\text{m}) \cdot (90.0\,\text{kPa})}{0.025\,\text{m} \cdot 2.85} \approx 40.8\,\text{mmHg}$$

Because peak systolic blood pressure is $120.0\,\text{mmHg} \gg 40.8\,\text{mmHg}$, the left ventricular free wall suffers catastrophic mechanical blowout within $72\,\text{hours}$.
