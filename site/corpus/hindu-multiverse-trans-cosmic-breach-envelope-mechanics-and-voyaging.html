3.3 Ontological Uniqueness of Gaṅgā in Hindu Epistemology

In Hindu ontology, all physical objects in our universe are transformations (Pariṇāma) of the 24 internal material elements (Pṛthvī through Mahat). However, the water of the Gaṅgā is classified by Gauḍīya and Puranic theologians as Brahmāṇḍa-bahir-varti-dravya (substance originating from beyond the cosmic egg):

  1. It is not condensed from intra-cosmic atmospheric vapor.
  2. It entered via a physical puncture (vivara) in the cosmic hull.
  3. Because it originates from the Causal Ocean that directly touches the transcendental body of Mahā-Viṣṇu, it retains eternal, indestructible spiritual potency that cannot be tainted by earthly decay.
